# s29 — a parking lot between lanes on their own levels: no bowl, no torn edges

Playtest (screenshot 53, Placemaker.log 15:06–15:09): a Parking build beside two existing lanes (Ironwood Lane,
Castlehill Lane) left a deep bowl in the middle of the lot. The asphalt draped down into it, a grass island slumped
into it, and the lot met the lanes with steep, torn edges. A wooden fence ran along the lanes. Every graded build
logged `terraform: N items … (direct brush)` and then `terraform reached its target in 0 pass(es), 0.0 s`
(106, 265 and 70 items).

## Causes

1. **The pass check called a deep hole "done".** `TerraformRaster.Residual.Done` accepted "at most 2 % of the inside
   texels off", however far off they were. A lot is graded to planes over terrain that already lies within 0.3 m of
   them almost everywhere. A bowl covering about 1 % of the texels therefore passed the very first measurement of
   the live terrain. No brush was ever applied, which explains `0 pass(es), 0.0 s`. The measurement itself was right:
   it reads the live CPU heights before any pass. The verdict was wrong.
   * The build then set `_tfVerified`. Nets and objects were placed at the raster's (graded) heights.
   * The surfaces followed the real ground down into the bowl, and the drive paths and islands at the planned
     height tore away from them.
2. **Lot edges ignored the roads' own heights.** LotGrading (s25) fitted its terraces to the raw terrain only.
   * Existing lanes sit on their own levels, on fill or in cuts.
   * At the outline, the graded lot ended at the terrain height, and the build's 4 m bank met the road at a
     different height: the torn edges.
3. **The fence is not part of the lot.** No Parking plan contains a fence net or fence piece (now asserted). The
   fences run along both sides of the lanes, so they belong to the lanes: a fenced road asset or an earlier build.
   Please confirm which, in game.

## Fixes

* **Pass check** (`Core/Build/TerraformRaster.cs`, `Game/Build/BuildService.Terrain.cs`)
  * `Residual.InsideMaxAbs` is now tracked.
  * `Done` = every texel within tolerance, or ≤ 2 % off and none of them more than 2.5 × the tolerance (0.75 m) off.
  * A bowl is now filled.
  * The success log names what the live terrain measured: `(measured on the live terrain: X/Y texels off, worst W m, rms R m)`.
* **Edges tied to the roads** (`Core/Layers/Parking/LotGrading.cs`, `Roads.cs`, `PlanBuilder.cs`)
  * `RoadLine` carries the road surface height (the captured curve), and `Roads.HeightAt` reads it.
  * Every 2 m along the outline, the road the edge faces is found by casting straight outward, not by taking the
    nearest road. If it is within its half width + 6 m, the outline point is tied to that road's height.
  * The ties take part in the tier fits (4× weight), so the planes lean towards the roads.
  * The remaining difference is blended in over a width that keeps it a gentle ramp: 1.5 × correction / 2.5 %,
    between 8 and 30 m. The blend uses Gaussian weights along the edge (σ 6 m), so a lot corner between two streets
    on different levels twists smoothly.
  * The blend is sampled once on a 2 m grid and emitted as 2 m sloped quads after the planar pieces.
  * Facts: `gradeTies`, `gradeMaxTie`.
* **Rows follow the ground the streets follow:** the cross-fall along the rows may now be up to 5 % (was 2 %; 5 % is
  the usual maximum for parking). Stalls stay level front to back (≤ 2 %) away from the edge blend.
* **No bowls:** the planned surface is tier planes, linear ramps and a smooth edge blend, so it has no local minima.
  With the pass check fixed, a hollow in the ground under the lot is filled, not left under the asphalt.

## Tests

`Tests/Placemaker.Tests/LotTieTests.cs`:
* **Pass check:** a 3 m hole covering 1 % of a raster is not done; 0.5 m noise on 1 % is done; filled live ground is
  done.
* **Lot between streets on their own levels:** the site is a gentle slope with a 3 m bowl in the middle, a street on
  1.2 m of fill to the south and one in a 0.8 m cut to the north. On the real raster:
  * the outline meets every adjacent road within ±0.3 m;
  * no hollow anywhere in the lot (3 m neighbourhood, 5 cm);
  * the bowl is filled by more than 2 m;
  * every stall stands on the graded ground;
  * no fence net or fence piece.

`LotGradingTests` (s25) is updated for the blend. On streets-on-all-sides sloped sites:
* stalls in the blend may fall up to 15 % and tilt up to 13 % (a ramping side row plus the blend);
* on cross and gentle ground at most a third of the stalls fall more than 2 %; on 8 % ground at most two thirds;
* rows count as tilted only above the 5 % cross-fall.

Full suite: 2831 passed. Mod build (`-p:Deploy=false`): 0 errors.
