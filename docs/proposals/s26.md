# s26 — cutouts that zone (A+ pass)

Playtests (log 14:56, images 44, 46–51): "zoned 4 of 16 cutout cell(s) … blocked 12"; lane-facing shop plots floating /
never zoned; cutouts of two roads crossing at angles; plots not flush; Shopping plaza lanes ending in dead-end caps;
every shop grew facing the existing street BEHIND the plaza's lane ("zoning only works when it grabs onto a road nearby,
not on a road we create").

## Causes

1. **Split edges move the grid.** Park paths connect to the streets and split their edges at the entrances; the game
   rebuilds both pieces' zone blocks with cells aligned to the new edge ends (BlockSystem.CreateBlocks centres whole
   cells on each piece's chord) — 1–2 m off the grid captured at design time. The blockers (0.5 m clear of the OLD
   cells) overlapped the new cells → blocked. Only a cutout on an edge no path touched survived (top-right, 4 cells).
2. **Lanes the build adds had no blocks at design time.** Their plots stayed unsnapped quads; blockers kept clear of
   the quad only; nothing told the game which road should zone them.
3. **Cutout enforcement cut the lanes.** ZoneGuard's "zoned cells reaching past a cutout" are cleared with
   `Cutouts.Enforce`, which clipped every net — including the lanes when a street's 48 m deep cell column crossed them —
   leaving dead-end caps (image 50). A cut lane no longer blocks the street's cells behind it, so the street behind
   zoned the plots and the shops faced it (images 49/51).
4. **A block column zoned several plots** (rows 0…deepest through the first plot into the next one) and the overlap
   test between blocks only checked cell centres → cutouts "cut into each other" at different angles.
5. **Alley zone blocks unverified.** Whether the vanilla Alley carries zone blocks was never measured (s12 / s14 open
   points). Now measured at catalog time (see below); if not, shop-fronting lanes use the Two-Lane Road.

## Fix

Core (`Build/ZoneGuard.cs`, new `Build/ZoneBlockPredictor.cs`, `Layers/Common/Cutouts.cs`, `Layers/Commercial/*`):
* `ZoneBlockPredictor` = port of `BlockSystem.CreateBlocks` (pieces by curvature, whole 8 m cells centred on each
  piece's chord, front edge at `GetCellWidth(width)·4` from the centre line, 6 deep, ≤ 10 cells per block; free ends,
  no continuous-edge / half-aligned cases). `ZoneGuard.Apply` adds the predicted blocks of every road-class net the
  plan adds (not when the catalog says the prefab has no zone blocks).
* The build's own ground nets (paths, lanes, aisles) close the cells under them and every cell behind (as the game).
* Blocks are tried in the order of the shallowest row at which they reach a cutout; ties go to the build's own
  roads (predicted / live-owned) — the plot faces its lane. A column zones ONE plot (stops where it ends). Blocks of
  two roads never zone overlapping land (polygon overlap, not centres). One Marquee rect per plot.
* A plot no block reaches (blocks are known around it) is dropped with a Design warning
  `Placemaker.Issue.CutoutNotZonable` and its land is blocked like the rest; a plot's second quad already covered by
  another road's cells is dropped silently.
* `Cutouts.Enforce` never clips road-class nets (lanes stay connected); other nets keep their attached objects.
* `ComposedPlan.ZoneQuads` keeps the original cutout quads; `ZoneGuard.Refresh(plan, liveGrids)` re-snaps blockers,
  zoning and preview cells to the live blocks with `ownBlockedOpen = false` (our paths now block for real).
* `Mall.ShopLane`: shop-fronting lanes (Shopping plaza U, Strip mall classic) use the Alley unless the catalog says
  it has no zone blocks (`AssetInfo.ZoneBlocks = 2`), then the Two-Lane Road (16 m, layout follows its width).
* `ParkCompanion` (s22) and fences (s24) unchanged.

Game:
* `BuildService`: new step **ZoneRefresh** after the main Apply (40 frames: split edges and new lanes get their
  blocks, cells re-checked) → capture live blocks (`SiteCapture.CaptureZoneBlocks`, cells + "ours") →
  `ZoneGuard.Refresh` → blockers + zoning from the refreshed plan. Logs `zoning refresh: N live block(s) (M of the
  build's own roads); C cutout cell(s) (planned P), K on a moved grid, Q zoning quad(s)` and per plot
  `zoning: plot i at (x, z) zones from 'Alley' (built by Placemaker) / 'Medium Road' (city) [(hidden there)]`.
* The 40-frame live check now names the cause of every blocked cutout cell: our zone block lot / our <role> net /
  behind a blocked cell / row 0 with row 1 blocked / (our) lot <prefab> / a city net or object, with examples and the
  offset from the plan.
* Catalog: `AssetInfo.ZoneBlocks` from `RoadData.m_ZoneBlockPrefab`; log `zone blocks by road: Alley yes|NO, …`.

Gallery: zoned cells are outlined (`--showcase`: commercial types show their lane cells flush with the lanes).
