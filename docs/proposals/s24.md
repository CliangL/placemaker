# s24 — borders follow the outline, not the zoning cutouts

Playtest (images 39/40): the park's brick wall with white railings ran round three sides of each zoning cutout — a
walled pen around the shops (Brookhaven Street and top right).

Cause: a host draws its border round its own region (`Fences.Runs(region …)` in Park/Campground, edge strips with
hedges), and that region has the cutouts notched out, so the border followed the notch. `Cutouts.Enforce` removed only
what lies *inside* a cutout, never what runs along its edge.

Fix: `Cutouts.ClearBorders` (run by `Cutouts.Enforce` at the end of `Composer.Compose`, with the area outline; and by
ZoneGuard's extra enforcement with the footprints): border nets (`NetRole.Fence`) are clipped where they come within
1 m of a cutout edge, and border objects (FencePiece, categories fence / hedge / bollard) within 1.5 m are dropped —
unless that spot is also within 1 m of the outer outline (the street side). Other nets keep their indices (attached
objects follow). No optional park/shops hedge was added (border between park and shops stays empty).

Tests: `CutoutBorderTests` — park with fence on and the Zoning feature (band/few, row/some, row/lots): no fence sample
within 0.9 m and no border object within 1 m of a cutout edge off the outline; the fence still runs along the outline;
a hand-made plan (fence round a notch → only the two outline runs survive; hedge on the notch dropped, on the outline
kept; path and bench untouched, bench index follows). Fails without the fix (fence at the notch).

In-game: build a park with Fence on and Zoning cutouts; the wall runs along the streets with gaps at the cutouts, no
wall around the cutout sides or back.
