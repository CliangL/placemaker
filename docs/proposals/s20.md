# s20 — terrain-aware water types: sculpted shores, quays, boardwalks, beaches into the water

Playtest feedback (screenshots 23, 24, 28–34): waterfronts "C+", the Wild dunes beach "F minus". Blocky terraced terrain,
paths looping into an "eye" island, switchbacks with a fence on every turn, benches scattered on slopes, planter rows in a
meadow, a beach whose sand stops at a pebble band short of the water, lumpy dunes, tilted pads and huts, disjointed raised
promenade segments with sand pyramids at the accesses, a promenade laid across a cliff face. Research: RESEARCH-NOTES-ADDENDUM
"Quays, piers and sculpted shores" (rules 67–71).

## What changed

**Terrain is a surface, not a staircase.**
- Contract (lead, please review): `PlannedTerraform.Sloped` (appended bool). A sloped patch carries its target in the ring's
  vertex heights; `Core/Build/TerraformSurface.At` interpolates them (mean-value coordinates). Every consumer handles it:
  `TerraformRaster.Build(…, vertexHeights, original)` (game direct path; the 4 m bank around a sloped patch now carries the
  patch's change instead of its absolute height — that made lips on slopes), `TerrainField.FromTerraform`, `SiteSafety` cuts,
  the overlay (sloped grids draw as one dashed outline), the gallery. Flat items behave as before; the dab fallback still levels.
- `Core/Layers/Common/Sculpt`: target heights on a 4 m node grid → sloped quads (shared corners = continuous), Smooth,
  LimitGrade, Pad/PadDisc, Corridor (level benches and ramps), FadeOut / CapToBorder (never steeper than 25 % towards ground it
  may not change), LimitEarthwork (cut/fill limits + Info issue), Roughness, Emit (Sutherland–Hodgman clipping, ctx.Ground
  registration), Resnap (objects, surfaces, terrain-following nets onto the final ground; HeightAfter uses the same MVC surface
  the build rasters, so planned heights equal built ground).
- `Core/Layers/Common/TerrainSurvey`: slope grid + classes (flat/gentle/steep/cliff), grade/relief helpers. Shared: any type can
  survey first. `Occupancy.GroundOk` (optional) keeps furniture off steep ground.

**The shoreline is the real one.**
- `Shoreline`: robust water level (median of ground + depth over clearly wet samples), level-trusted wet test (terrain below the
  level next to water), outline probes out to 34 m, the measured waterline in front of every station (`Gap`, bisected, smoothed,
  up to 45 m past the outline), bank kind per station (`ShoreKind` low / bank / cliff from the profile), `FromWater`, `WaterAt`,
  `WaterEdge`, `OffsetFromWater` (loop-free: `RemoveLoops` cuts swallowtails, short gaps bridged, smoothed), `StripFromWater`,
  `BandFromWater`, `SelfIntersections`. Game: the water snapshot is sampled at 8 m (was 16 m).
- `Types/Beach/ShoreGround`: the terrain step every water type runs first — sculpt over land + foreshore with cliffs locked (the
  cliff face out to where the plateau flattens), profile by distance from the real water, Settle (smooth, limits, fade, grade),
  MakeLevel pads, ProfileAlong/Bench/Walk (a walk is trimmed where its level would climb steeply to ground it may not change, so it
  never ends in a ramp up the border), Ramp, Connect (entrance → walk as ONE constant-grade ramp, one diagonal when too steep,
  never a tour across the park), Runs (stretches by bank kind), Finish (emit, resnap, drop any furniture left on > 15 %).
- `Types/Beach/CliffTop`: cliff stretches get a walk on the plateau set back from the edge, lookouts (paved landing, benches
  facing the sea, a railing only where the ground drops, a lamp), railings along the walk only at real drops. A shore that is
  mostly cliff gets a Design (Warning) issue `Placemaker.Shore.Issue.Cliff` and builds only that.

**Beach** (ids stable). Profile from the real water: an underwater shelf (sand painted at full strength ~20 m under the water —
new control "Sand under water", default 20 m), the beach face at "Beach slope", a graded bank (≤ ~25 %) into the ground behind,
low smooth dune ridges parallel to the shore (smooth cross profile × slow 90 m swell, no noise lumps); ONE promenade on a level
bench; accesses are straight graded ramps to level landings; huts/kiosks/courts on level pads (or skipped); dune fences off by
default (vanilla has no timber sand fence: the pool picked brick walls with railings). Presets retuned (dune heights 1.5–2 m).

**Waterfront** (ids stable, labels renamed): the edge element is new control "Water edge" (auto by style):
| preset id | label | edge | design |
|---|---|---|---|
| promenade | Promenade boardwalk | timber boardwalk (LeisurePier01Small01) 2.6 m off the bank at water + 1 m | lawn embankment, paved promenade terrace 14 m back, benches facing the water every 14 m, lamps between, planted beds, tree row, park; diagonal links ≤ 10 % with timber stubs |
| pier | Pier park | walkway quay (QuaySmall02) | promenade terrace above a lawn bank; 2 piers with T-heads starting from paved landings, one ≤ 10 % ramp each |
| harbor | Harbour quay | wide quay (QuaySmallWide02) at water + 1.8 m | apron filled to deck level and paved, lamps and benches along the quay, kiosks with cafe seats, avenue, one pier on the quay |
| riverSteps | River steps | low walkway quay (water + 0.9 m) | promenade embankment ≥ 1.5 m above it, 3 paved step flights (deliberate levels) down to the quay, double avenue |
| fishing | Natural bank | none: bank graded into the water | riprap, fishing spots on small level landings, gravel walk, dirt paint at the waterline |
Fences only where the ground drops > 1 m (quays bring their own railing); the flower-bed / big-tree features are no longer on by
default (they scattered planter rows in the lawn); beds are planted by the layer. New controls (appended): edge, deckHeight,
quayAsset (`net.quay`), boardwalkAsset (`net.pier`), beds; steps default none; railings default off.

**Shared code touched (owners please check):** `Core/Contracts.cs` (Sloped), `Core/Build/TerraformRaster.cs` (vertex heights,
original), `Core/Build/SiteSafety.cs` (sloped cuts), `Core/Layers/Common/TerrainField.cs` (sloped lookup), `Occupancy.GroundOk`,
`LayerBase.TrimOutlineEnds/KeepPathEndsInside` skip explicit-height decks (a quay or boardwalk is built at the water, which may lie
a few metres past the outline), `Game/Build/BuildService.Terrain.cs` (vertex heights + original ground into the raster),
`Game/Overlay` (sloped outline), `Game/Preview/SiteCapture.cs` (8 m water grid).

## Tests

`Tests/Placemaker.Tests/Types/ShoreSculptTests.cs` with sloped shore fixtures (curved coast, flat / 6.5 m bank / headland / 13 m
cliff, a game-like 8 m bilinear water snapshot): MVC interpolation exact and continuous; a sloped grid rasters without steps;
offset lines never loop; the waterline is found from the terrain within 1.5 m with the outline 12 m short of the water; for every
beach and waterfront preset on flat / bank / headland sites: objects on the final ground (± 0.3 m), furniture never on > 16 %,
paths without self-intersections or crossings away from junctions, path grades ≤ 20 % and never on a cliff face, fences only at
drops > 1 m, no stepped terraform (slope change per metre), no local peaks, earthwork within limits, every terraform patch sloped
or a deliberate level; quays within ±2.2 m of the real water edge; sand ≥ 0.85 at and 4–10 m under the water (outline on or
short of the water) and a graded beach face; cliff shores natural (no terraform / props / paths / sand on the face) with a
cliff-top walk and lookouts and the Design issue; the headland builds on its low shore only; 5 ha on slopes within budget.
Older beach/waterfront tests updated to the new design (distances from the real water, no default sand fences, quay/boardwalk
assertions).

## Proposals (not done)

- A real stair net/asset for steep links (vanilla has none for paths; links use one ≤ 10 % diagonal ramp, river steps use levels).
- Moored boats along the harbour quay (shore kit has `shore.boat`): skipped to keep the quay edge clean until verified in game.
- The other types still emit flat terraform cells via `TerrainShaper`; switching it to `Sculpt` would remove their terraces too.
