# s34 — the Roads overhaul: one connected network, managed main roads, walkways, pre-zoning, terrain fit

Playtest (images 59–63, a "village" on a hill): whole street systems and single streets floating connected to nothing
(Arborview, Kent, the Pearl/Bellwood/Sunset… cluster around the ruin); the main street with a junction every 40–60 m;
the edge-zoning feature zoning a scattered pattern of cells; the network ignoring the hill; cul-de-sac bulbs side by
side with no path between them; odd clipped corners. Asks: no disconnected roads, pedestrian paths everywhere (dirt,
paved, random), alleys, fewer avenue connections (slider + defaults), pre-zone all new roads, follow the contours (or
not), dirt roads at the tips, better corners, many more presets.

## Why the islands happened

1. **Stub pruning cascaded.** After obstacle removal (`RoadObstacles`) and again after attaching to existing roads, the
   planner ran `RoadNetwork.PruneStubs`: a node that "had ≥ 2 edges and now has 1" loses its edge, repeatedly. On a
   tree-shaped layout (organic, superblock paths) it ran through whole networks — the gallery's rural village on a site
   with a farm lost every road. Now nothing prunes there; dead ends are decided once, at the end (below).
2. **The old rule kept loose networks on purpose** (`DropFragments`: "an unconnected new district is a legitimate plan").
3. **Roads too close to buildings were dropped after the layer.** Obstacle avoidance tested the centre line with a 4 m
   margin; the composer's site safety drops any net whose *body* (half width + 0.5 m) touches a lot. A 16 m street 6 m
   from a farmhouse survived the layer and was removed by the composer, leaving islands and bare ends. Buildings now
   keep the road's half width + 1 m + the margin (`RoadObstacles.Blocked(curve, halfWidth)`), for links and walks too.

## Pipeline (Core/Layers/Roads)

`RoadsPlanner`: generate → (follow contours: `RoadContourWarp`) → split at existing roads → obstacles → perimeter →
remove overlaps → attach → **`RoadDesign.Run`** → output nets/caps/paint → **pre-zone** → regions.

`RoadDesign.Run` (one file per pass):

| pass | file | does |
|---|---|---|
| merge crowded junctions | `RoadTopology.MergeCloseJunctions` | junction–junction edges < 18 m collapse (attached nodes never move) |
| access management | `RoadAccess` | side junctions on main roads (avenues) thinned to **Junction spacing**: kept by importance (city links, 4-way crossings, side network served), the rest cut back into a cul-de-sac before the avenue (35 m setback) or dropped; a cut that strands a branch is relinked to its neighbours (never onto the avenue within the spacing) or undone |
| connectivity | `RoadConnectivity.Ensure` | car components via union-find; anchored = touches the city. Each loose piece gets the best clean straight link (no crossing, ≥ 2 road widths clear of other roads, around buildings/water, inside the area, 60–120° meetings, grade ≤ 1.3 × design grade, never onto a managed main road); then a link to the city; then a relaxed second try (longer/steeper, scored); then two loose pieces join; else dropped with an Info issue. Big plans get 1 city link per ~450 m of outline, 180 m apart |
| steep links (follow contours) | `RoadDesign.PruneSteep` | junction-to-junction streets > 1.15 × design grade go, steepest first, while the network stays one piece (cul-de-sacs stay) |
| hairpins | `RoadTopology.RemoveHairpins` | a road doubling back at a plain node (> 110°, e.g. a perimeter cut by an existing road hooking into a street) loses its short side |
| dead ends | `RoadConnectivity.CleanDeadEnds` | an unplanned dead end within 70 m of an existing road joins it (one a few metres short simply snaps onto it); stubs < 40 m go; longer ones become cul-de-sacs (turning circle); two tips facing each other < 60 m apart are joined |
| back lanes / grid paths | `RoadWalkways.Lanes` | through the middle of blocks between two parallel roads 60–195 m apart, along the network's main axis (junction-to-junction chords; on a slope the family that climbs least, i.e. along the contours), T-ing in at mid-block, never onto an avenue: Alley / Gravel Road / mixed; blocks without a lane may get a mid-block footpath |
| square junctions | `RoadTopology.SquareJunctions` | a side street meeting another road under 62° is bent (first control point, ≤ 24 m arm) to the middle of its free angle; a road passing through never bends |
| round bends | `RoadTopology.FilletBends` | plain nodes turning > 14° get a real curve, centre-line radius by class (street ≥ 22 m, avenue ≥ 45 m, lane ≥ 10 m) — the "car cut-off" corners |
| crumbs | `RoadNetwork.CollapseShortEdges(8)` | pieces < 8 m merge |
| gravel tips | `RoadWalkways.GravelTips` | Cul-de-sac road: same / gravel / some gravel — the dead-end run from its last junction becomes `Gravel Road` (small turning circle) |
| walkways | `RoadWalkways.Build` | (1) each access cut keeps a footpath to the main road; (2) every cul-de-sac gets a path from its **neck** (outside the turning circle, square to the street) to the street or close it saves the longest walk to (road distance / straight ≥ 1.8–4.5 by density); (3) long blocks get mid-block cut-throughs (rays from the middle of chains ≥ 170–420 m, only where the way round is ≥ 2.2× longer). Joins split the road edge (a join within 9 m of an edge end uses the end node); never a free end, never onto a bulb or a dead end, never crossing a road or walk, clear of other roads, ≤ 8 % (vanilla has no stairs net) |
| loose paths | `RoadConnectivity.PruneLoosePaths` | car-free pieces with a free end go (CS2 deletes stray path stubs) |
| grades | `RoadDesign.Grades` | steepest 16 m window per car edge; > design grade → Warning `Steep` at the spot (6 shown), stat "Steepest road" |

Walk style: **paved** (Pavement Path / the Footpath pick), **dirt trails** (category `net.trail` with the park trail
name fallbacks — a modded gravel path when the playset has one — plus a 5 m feathered dirt `PlannedPaint` corridor),
**mixed**. `RoadsPlanner.TrailPaint` is the hook for s33's terrain-paint toolkit (painted trails) once merged.

## Terrain fit (`terrainFit`: auto | follow | ignore, `maxGrade` 4–16 %, default 10)

* **Follow contours** — grid / superblocks: aligned to the dominant contour direction (AlignToTerrain), then
  `RoadContourWarp` lays every long street (runs along the axis, subdivided every 30 m) onto one contour: nodes slide
  across the slope (≤ ⅓ block, smoothed along the street) to the run's median height and the street is redrawn as one
  smooth curve; cross streets follow. Only on a real hillside or valley (slope tensor anisotropy > 0.3): on a round hill the
  contours are rings a grid cannot follow, so the grid stays straight and loses its too-steep links instead. Reverted when
  anything would cross. Organic: the walker steers by the terrain
  (`OrganicBuilder.TerrainSteer`: turn toward the contour just enough to stay under 90 % of the design grade, light pull
  along contours once a branch is a block away from its parent; branches leave diagonally where the climb is gentlest;
  loops straight up the hill are refused). Tree: collector along the contours on a real hillside (slope tensor
  anisotropy > 0.35, not on a round hill). Loop: aligned. Contour pattern: as before, plus the passes above.
* **Ignore terrain** — the strict geometry whatever the per-pattern toggle says. **Auto** — the old behaviour.
* The GRG generators are unchanged when the new parameters are off (`FollowContours`, `MaxGrade`,
  `MainJunctionSpacing`): the golden replay test still passes.

Organic main street: side branches no closer than the junction spacing (`GridParameters.MainJunctionSpacing`), and a
branch may leave an existing junction on the other side — crossroads instead of staggered T's.

## Pre-zone new roads (`preZone`: none | zone kinds, replaces the edge-zoning feature)

* Core `RoadPreZone`: one Marquee quad per predicted zone block (`ZoneBlockPredictor`) of every zoning road the plan adds
  (both sides, 1 m inside the outer cells), fact `roads.preZone`.
* Game `BuildService.PreZone.cs`: new step **RoadPreZone** after the main Apply of such a (loose) build: 40 frames, then
  the live blocks around the build with the curve of their owner edge (`CaptureOwnedBlocks`); blocks whose owner edge
  lies on a planned road (ends + middle within 1.5 m of one planned curve: `RoadPreZone.FromLive`) are zoned whole —
  splits and junctions the prediction could not know are exact. Other layers' zoning is kept as planned; without a live
  match the planned quads are used. Marquee never overwrites the player's zoning; blocked cells stay empty.
  Log: `roads pre-zoning: N live zone block(s) around the build, M of the new roads zoned whole (zone)`.
* **Old saves**: a roads step with the old edge-zoning feature child (target "edge") is migrated on load
  (`RoadsLayer.Migrate`, called from `LayerAliases.Normalize`): enabled → `preZone` = its zone type; the child goes.

## Controls (ids are new, nothing renamed)

`junctionSpacing` (0–250 m, 120, Essential), `walkways` (0–100 %, 50, Essential), `walkwayStyle`, `backLanes` (0–100 %, 0),
`laneStyle`, `laneAsset` (Automatic: Alley → Gravel Road → Two-Lane), `tipStyle`, `terrainFit` (Essential), `maxGrade`,
`preZone` (Essential, options read "Placemaker.Zone.<kind>"). `pathAsset` ("Footpath") is shown for every pattern now
(walkways use it). Group headings `walkways`, `terrain`. Legend role **lane** (role Alley in a roads plan; colour
#c9a27a; `PreviewLegend` maps a roads layer's alleys to it — one-line change in Core/Build, lead please review).
No contract change (schema-driven), PROTOCOL unchanged.

## Tests (`RoadsDesignTests`, 20)

Every pattern (9) and preset (12) on flat / hill / valley / steep road sites (two existing streets, a farm): one
network joined to the city, no bare dead ends (with turning circles on), no loose walk ends, no pieces < 6 m, no errors;
islands cut by a building rejoin; link-or-drop on a synthetic network; dead ends capped or pruned; ladder: kept main-road
junctions ≥ 150 m apart and the cut streets end in cul-de-sacs; planned grids with an avenue respect 180 m; cul-de-sac
footpaths leave at the neck and keep the bulb; walks never cross roads, ≤ 8 %; lanes never meet an avenue, gravel lanes;
gravel tips + trail paint; pre-zone quads = predicted blocks, default none; live refresh keeps only the new roads' blocks
and covers every cell centre; old edge-zoning feature migrates (enabled / disabled, idempotent); steep issues located;
follow contours makes no more steep roads than ignore (grid, organic × hill, valley, steep) and the contour grid climbs
≥ 10 % less; bends rounded; side streets squared; crowded junctions merged; presets valid and labelled.
Updated: `FeatureTests` (roads has no features; pre-zone test), `RoadsRolesTests` (walkways in the legend by default).

## Known limits / next

* Access management keeps a junction when cutting it would strand a branch nobody can reach otherwise (tree layouts).
* Organic on a round hilltop leaves the summit empty (no branch can climb there under the design grade).
* No stairs: walkways steeper than 8 % are skipped. Dirt trails are a paint corridor + the best path net the playset has.
* Old town (mixed pattern) is a radial circus with organic lanes, not a dense medieval mesh.
* In game to check: the pre-zone log line; walkways joining at the bulb neck keep the turning circle object valid; gravel
  tips' small turning circles.
