# s22 — calmer preview, a legend, cutouts that stay off the road and actually zone

Playtest (curved park between four streets, Design preview): "It's getting crazy; hard to even see what's happening.
That blue-green square in the bottom right — is that sticking into the road? The red squares are a lot to look at. The
orange dashed terrain boxes are hard to look at — shouldn't it be a terrain heat map? I need a legend … hover an entry
and it hides everything except that thing." Lead, after building the same park (zoning view): no zone cells at all on
the park side of the four streets, not even in the two cutouts.

## 1. After the build: no cutout cells (lead's bug)

Log (13:02): `1 zone block lot(s) keep zoned buildings off group 1`, no line about zoning, `left out 188 rejected parts
(SteepSlope, attempt 1)`, `terraform incomplete (132/1124 cells still off after 4 passes)`.

Findings (decompiled 1.6.2f1 `CellBlockJobs`, `ZoneUtils`, `Net/Objects ValidationHelpers`):

* **One blocker lot is expected**: footprint − cutout cells is one ring with notches (no holes). The game's cell test is
  exact (cell quad shrunk 0.02 m vs. each lot triangle), ZoneGuard's cell geometry matches `ZoneUtils` exactly, and the
  blocker keeps 0.5 m off every zoned cell — the blocker as planned cannot block a cutout cell (new test, and the build
  now logs it: `zone blocking: N lot ring(s), X m², keeping M cutout cell(s) free [— K touched (bug)]`).
* **Most likely cause: ZoneGuard zoned cells the game hides.** Where blocks overlap (curves, junction corners, split
  edges) the game shows only one block's cells (`CellFlags.Visible`). s19 captured blocks without their cells, so it
  could pick the hidden block's cells as the cutout: the Marquee zoning then finds no visible cell (zones nothing), and
  the blocker covers the *visible* block's cells on the same land (they are not in the zoned set) → the whole cutout is
  blocked: no grid, no zone — exactly the screenshot. Fix: `SiteCapture` now captures each block's cell flags
  (`ZoneGrid.Cells`: Visible / Blocked); hidden cells are never used, and a piece of land is zoned by one block only.
* **The park companion sat in a cutout.** `ParkCompanionPlanner` puts the hidden park building on the road-side spot
  farthest from the build's objects — an empty cutout is exactly that, and a building takes the cells it stands on. The
  zoning quads are now obstacles for it (`CutoutPoints`).
* **Zoning was silent.** A zone prefab the playset lacks skipped the quad without a word. Now: `zoning: N quad(s)
  emitted`, a warning naming missing zone prefabs, and 40 frames after the zoning Apply a check of the live cells:
  `zoning: group G: zoned Z of N cutout cell(s) (Q quads); empty E, blocked B, hidden H, not found F` (warning when
  Z < N) — the next playtest tells which step loses them if any still does.
* **"188 rejected parts (SteepSlope)" is mislabelled.** `SteepSlope` is a net-only error (edge end heights vs. the
  prefab's max slope, `Net.ValidationHelpers`); the retry excludes every errored temporary but named only the first
  error type found. The ghost of the same plan dropped 196 optional objects for collisions — the 188 are almost surely
  those (overlapping trees/props) plus a few path edges on terrain that was still moving (terraform incomplete: 12 %
  of the raster cells off by > 0.3 m after 4 passes, typically the pond bank / sculpted slopes). The log now gives the
  split and a histogram: `left out N rejected parts (nets n, objects o, surfaces s, areas a; errors: OverlapExisting×…,
  SteepSlope×…)`. Not changed: the terraform convergence itself (needs an in-game measurement first).

## 2. Cutout across Juniper Street (preview)

Cause: a column of *another* road's block (a road on the far side of the curved street; blocks reach 48 m) passed
through the cutout quad. ZoneGuard zoned rows 0 … the cutout row of any block whose land in front of the cutout lay
outside the park — "the strip between outline and kerb" — and the curved road counted as such a strip. Rotated like the
other road, across the sidewalk and the carriageway: the screenshot.

Fix (`Core/Build/ZoneGuard.cs`): a column ends at its first closed cell — a cell whose centre is within half width +
3.5 m of a captured road/track (`site.NearbyNets`; row 0 sits 4 m off the edge, so never caught), a hidden cell, or a
cell the game blocks outside the build — and every cell behind it is closed too (vanilla CleanBlockedCells). A blocked
cell *inside* the footprint stays usable (that is the build being edited). Overlapping blocks zone a piece of land once
(first block wins). Closed cells are not listed at all (never drawn). Blocked cells carry `ZoneCellMark.Clipped` = the
part inside the outline: the preview never draws over sidewalks or roads.

## 3. Calmer preview (overlay)

* Outline, paths/roads, features read first; trees are a faint crown (10 % fill, thin rim, small trunk dot); planting
  small soft discs; benches/lamps/bins one small lilac dot or bar; surfaces a faint tint with a thin outline.
* Terraform = **heat map** (`Core TerrainHeat`): height change (target − ground before the build), sampled on a ≤ 6000
  cell grid (≥ 3 m) inside the outline, bands ±0.25 / 0.75 / 1.5 / 3 m; fill orange → red, cut light → deep blue, alpha
  10 – 31 % by band, nothing under 0.25 m, rectangles clipped to the outline. No dashed boxes, no sloped outline.
* Cutouts: zone colour fill 22 % + thin outline. Blocked-for-zoning: off by default; when shown one 9 % tint, clipped,
  no hatch/outline. Decals: off by default.

## 4. Legend window

* A small window at the bottom of the panel, on the side the flyouts open (left of the docked panel), collapsible
  (remembered). Rows: swatch, name, one number (length / area / count; terrain "−1.2 / +0.8 m"), eye. Roads are a
  "Roads" group (the s16 "On the map" legend moved here, with their prefab names).
* Hover a row → the map draws only that layer (fills of the others go to alpha 0, meshes kept: instant). Leaving the
  window ends it. Click → hide / show (kept for the game session, `PlacemakerToolSystem.Legend`, static). "All" → every
  layer back to its default. Design and Review only.
* Every drawn element has exactly one layer: `PreviewLegend.Of(object | net(+layer generator) | surface | area | paint
  | terraform | zoning | zone cell)`. Layers: paths, road:<role>, features, trees, planting, water, surfaces, objects,
  parking, entrances, terrain, cutouts, blocked, decals.
