# s14 — Zoning cutouts + the Commercial category

User (playtest): the "Street shops" feature should be **Zoning**, any zone type, default low density commercial.
Then (direction change): "it should just literally be cutouts for zoning. No fanciness." — empty land along the
street, nothing placed in it (the screenshot had picnic tables and a tent prop in the strip). Everything elaborate
(strip malls, lots + alleys + shops, parks woven with shops) moves to a new **Commercial** category of types.

## 1. The Zoning feature (parks, plazas, market squares, leisure types)

Feature layer id `edgeZoning`, target `shops`, label now "Zoning". Controls (ids unchanged = save format):

| where | id | values | note |
|---|---|---|---|
| host (feature options) | `shopAmount` | none · few · some · lots | label "Amount" |
| host | `shopLayout` | band (Row) · pads (Speckled) | label "Pattern". s12's `clusters`, `inward`, `lane` are retired: they load as **Row** (`LayerBase.MigrateValue`, used by `Merge` and by `DesignEditor.ApplyDefaults`) |
| host | `shopDepth` | 8–48 m, whole cells | label "Depth" |
| feature child | `zoneKind` (new) | commercialLow (default) · commercialHigh · mixed · residentialLow · residentialRow · residentialMedium · residentialHigh · lowRent · officeLow · officeHigh · industrial | label "Zone"; `Core/Layers/Common/ZoneKinds.cs` |
| feature child | `zone` | zone asset | now "Zone asset" (More tier); when set it overrides `zoneKind` (mod zones) |
| feature child | `depth` | 8–48 m | hidden inside cutouts (`VisibleWhen "cutout!=true"`; the feature preset sets `cutout=true`); a cutout is zoned to its full depth |

**Bug fixed on the way:** `PlannedZoning.ZoneId = ""` zones *nothing* in game (`PlanWork` skips empty ids), and
`zone` defaulted to "" — so s12's street shops (and the Roads layer's edge zoning) never zoned a cell in game unless a
zone asset was picked. `zoneKind` always resolves to a prefab now. Roads' edge zoning therefore defaults to low
commercial too (its own feature preset could pick residential — roads owner's call).

**Zone prefabs** (catalog dump 1.6.2f1, vanilla): themed `EU|NA Commercial Low/High`, `EU|NA Residential
Low/Medium/Medium Row/High/Mixed`; neutral `Residential LowRent`, `Office Low/High`, `Industrial Manufacturing`.
The unprefixed `Commercial Low`… prefabs have no localised name (legacy; likely no buildings) — not used. Plans name
the EU prefab; `AssetCatalogSystem.Resolve` swaps the prefix for the city theme (`CityConfigurationSystem.defaultTheme`
→ `ThemePrefab.assetPrefix`) when that prefab exists, the EU one stays as fallback.

**Cutouts stay empty** — `Core/Layers/Common/Cutouts.cs`, called once at the end of `Composer.Compose` (lead: one
additive line in `Composition.cs`). A cutout = a region tagged `shops` + `place:shops` (the feature's plots and the
Commercial types' plots). Every layer except `edgeZoning` loses objects whose footprint reaches in (trees 1.6 m, others
0.35 m), net stretches inside (nets are split, attachments kept at the original ends), and surface/area parts inside
(boolean difference, re-made acceptable). Found by the new test before the fix: kiosk (`shops`) and restroom ground
pads, golf/dog-park ground, a festival-camping path. The edgeZoning planner ignores reserved space inside a cutout (a
sibling's leak must not shorten the zoned rows).

## 2. The Commercial category (Make-row group `commercial`)

`Core/Layers/Commercial/`: `Mall.cs` (kit), `CommercialLayer.cs` (base, shared controls), one file per type.
Recipes `Recipes/{stripMall,shoppingPlaza,outdoorMall,shopsPark}.json`, Lang `Lang/en-US.commercial.json`,
UI: `MAKE_GROUPS` gained `commercial` (after Parking), `makeGroupOf` guesses mall/retail/shopping ids.

The kit composes real pieces, never re-invents them:
* **lanes** — vanilla `Alley` (8 m) or `Pedestrian Street` (16 m) / `Pedestrian Street Small` (8 m), joined to an
  **existing** street at their ends (`StreetShops.StreetEnd`; a parent-planned street cannot take a mid-segment
  junction). Lane junctions keep 12 m from street corners.
* **plots** (= cutouts) — whole 8 m cells, ≤ 3 cells per plot along the row, each must face a street or a lane
  (`Mall.Faces`, the edgeZoning test); zoned by running the **edgeZoning planner** on each plot.
* **parking lots** — the **Parking layer** planner on a cut (`Mall.Lot`): aisles, bays, lamps, planting, EV chargers.
  Street entrances are ordinary parking entrances (connector to the kerb). Lane entrances use PLT's `alley` entrance
  kind: the lane is handed to the sub-plan as a synthetic `NearbyNet`, the parking planner builds its short alley
  stub from the lane's centre line, then the synthetic attachment is dropped and the lane is **split at the stub's
  end** (identical node). The lot's own frontage zoning is forced off.
* **green / plaza pieces** — the **Park**, **Plaza** and **Market square** planners as sub-plans on what is left
  (`Mall.Rest` slits pieces with holes, e.g. green round a lot, so no planner plans over the hole); path ends at
  "doors" (a lane's dead end) are snapped onto the lane node.
* **leftovers** — split by a morphological opening (18 m): the wide parts become pocket parks, the thin arms (beside
  lane legs, behind rows) a planted buffer (lawn + a loose tree line, no paths), so no park path hugs the outline.

| type | presets | composition |
|---|---|---|
| **Strip mall** `stripMall` | classic · corner (corner retail) · rearAlley · neighbourhood · bigBox | classic: street → lot → U lane → shop row facing the lot → pocket park. corner: lot on the street corner, L lane from street to street round it, shops wrap the L, street rows continue. rearAlley: shops on the street, service U lane behind, lot behind the lane entered from it |
| **Shopping plaza** `shoppingPlaza` | plaza · market (market hall) · court (parking court) · green | U lane round a central court (Plaza / Market square / Parking / Park planner); shop rows outside the U all face the court across the lane; side lots on the remaining frontage, else a lot behind off the street at the back |
| **Outdoor mall** `outdoorMall` | promenade · grand · loop · village | pedestrian shopping street with plots both sides ending on a plaza; side lots; loop = pedestrian loop with plots on both sides round a central square |
| **Shops & park** `shopsPark` | clusters · facingIn · alley · speckled · row | s12's layouts (StreetShops: row, speckled, clusters, facing in, alley) + an optional small car park, the park (chosen style) on the rest |

Shared controls (labels `Placemaker.Commercial.*`): `zoneKind`, `plotDepth` (8–40 m), parking `lotSize`
(small/medium/large = 1/2/3 aisles), `stallAngle`, `lotGreen` (lot trees), `evShare`, `lamps`. Type controls: strip mall
`style`, `frontage`; shopping plaza `court`, `courtWidth`, `courtDepth`, `sideLots`; outdoor mall `style`, `plazaSize`,
`spineLength`, `sideLots`; shops & park `layout`, `amount`, `parkStyle`, `lot`. Features: decals (on), flatten terrain.
Without an existing street a commercial type plans a pocket park and warns (`Commercial.Issue.NoStreet`).

### Controls that should become "disabled with reason" once s15 lands

Today they use `VisibleWhen` (hidden) or are simply ignored:
* shops & park: `stallAngle`, `lotGreen`, `evShare`, `lamps` while `lot=none` (hidden now via `lot=small`).
* strip mall: `frontage` with `style=corner` (hidden now).
* shopping plaza / outdoor mall: `lotSize`, `stallAngle`, `lotGreen`, `evShare`, `lamps` while `sideLots=false`
  (shopping plaza `court` style still uses them) — shown now.
* outdoor mall: `plazaSize` / `spineLength` mean slightly different things for `loop` (court size / depth) — shown now.
* Zoning feature: `shopLayout` and `shopDepth` while `shopAmount=none` — shown now.

## Tests

* `ZoningCutoutTests` — every type hosting the Zoning feature × each of its other features alone, all at once (Row
  and Speckled): no object, net or ground of any layer in a cutout.
* `CommercialTests` — every commercial type × every preset × 4 sites (1.3 ha, 2.6 ha, wide 2.4 ha, L-shape): no
  errors; every plot zoned, faces a street or lane, plots never overlap; zoning only on plots with a real zone id;
  lanes attach to real streets at their ends (no synthetic ids leak), never run over a plot; bays and drive lanes keep
  off plots; lots are entered; nothing stands on a plot; surfaces accepted. Plus determinism, zone type → ZoneId
  (commercial types and the feature, explicit asset override), theme swap, lot stubs share a node with the lane,
  no-street fallback, category/presets.
* `StreetShopsTests` updated: the feature is Row/Speckled only, never plans alleys; retired values load as Row.

## Open problems / engine questions

1. **Vanilla Alley / Pedestrian Street zone blocks** — assumed (PLT built zoning roads from Alley; CS2 pedestrian
   streets are zoneable). Verify in game that plots along a planned lane get blocks once the lane is built (plots are
   zoned in the same build, after the nets).
2. **Which way a plot faces** is CS2's choice where a plot lies between a street and a lane (rear alley shops); valid
   either way.
3. **Zone theme swap** reads `ThemePrefab.assetPrefix` of the city's default theme; untested in game (falls back to
   the EU prefab).
4. Parking-lot entrances on a lane are alley stubs built in the same batch as the lane — the same mechanism as the
   Parking layer's alley entrances, but onto a new net instead of an existing one; verify the junction forms.
5. Plan time: 50–400 ms per type on 1–2.5 ha in the gallery (Parking + Park sub-plans); within budget, but the Park
   sub-plan dominates.
6. Commercial types need an existing street; on a site cut out of a Roads recipe they fall back to a park.
