# s18 — small plazas that look designed, and ground palettes

Playtest feedback (in-game screenshot, ~0.15 ha triangle between two streets): loud orange-red brick bands, mustard
fill and a maroon outline band; a grey sub-plaza whose rings cut across the border bands and each other; a 9 m
fountain tower half over the circle's edge; grass slivers between bands; lamps and benches unrelated to the edges.
"Really small plazas need to be optimised — even micro plazas should still look good. I don't love the orange and
yellow. It should be part of the presets — self-aware of the colour palette — and you could swap palettes in a ground
setting, like the road mixes."

## 1. Size tiers (Core/Layers/Plaza/PlazaCompact.cs)

| tier | rule (real metres) | composition |
|---|---|---|
| micro | area < 450 m² or inscribed radius < 6 m | field + one thin border band (0.7–1 m) + one focal piece (fountain / small fountain / tree / pots) + lamps in the corners |
| small | area < 2000 m² or inscribed radius < 12 m | + focal pad, bench ring facing the focal piece (symmetric pairs, gaps toward the entrances), corner trees; a long strip becomes an allée (trees on the spine, benches between) |
| medium | < 8000 m² | full composition (PlazaDressing), with the fit rules below |
| large | ≥ 8000 m² | full composition, kerbed walks |

* **Focal point** = `LeisureKit.FocalPoint`: the centroid when it is ≥ 92 % as deep as the shape allows, else the
  near-deepest point closest to the centroid (incentre of a triangle, middle of a strip's medial line). The square
  centres its pad here, and **FountainAddOn tries the focal point first** (all sizes with seating, then alone,
  then the old "most central free spot"), so fountain and pad always agree. Reserved places (street-shop land,
  amenity pads) are subtracted first on both sides.
* The pad is laid to the fountain the add-on *will* build (`FountainAddOn.PredictApron`), and the plaza's fountain
  feature now uses the automatic large kit (first item LargeFountain02) instead of a hard-coded asset, so a small
  square gets a small fountain instead of a tower that does not fit. The square lights itself (feature lamps off).
* Small squares give no land to street shops (`LayerBase.StreetShopsFit`, Info issue) — they would eat the square.
* Levels on small squares use narrow flights/ramps; the amphitheatre preset still tries its bowl.

**Fit rules for medium/large** (PlazaDressing.Ground): the rondel ring and outer inlay ring are only laid when they
stay a clear metre inside the frame band and inside the border inlay line; otherwise the outer ring, then the border
line, are dropped and the pad shrinks. Walks end at the rondel's ring and cut through the outer ring (laid before it),
so no path stub is left between rings. Dark walk kerbs only on large squares. Lawns keep the frame band + 1 m clear;
scatter slabs keep clear of every ring.

**No slivers** (PavingKit.PavingPlan.Emit): design pieces under 2.5 m² or 0.55 m mean width are not laid *and not
claimed*, so the field paves them (before, a dropped sliver left bare ground).

Tests: `Tests/Placemaker.Tests/PlazaSizeTests.cs` — 5 shapes (triangle, slim triangle, sliver trapezoid, square, L)
× 5 sizes (250 m² … 1.5 ha) + every preset on triangles/slivers at 300 and 1500 m²: no ground pieces overlapping
(> 0.5 m²), no piece < 1.2 m² or thinner than 0.3 m, paving covers ≥ 93 % of the square, nothing straddles the
outline, the fountain on the centre of the focal pad; small squares use ≤ 3 stones, no lawns/paths, one focal piece.

## 2. Ground palettes (Core/Layers/Common/GroundPalettes.cs)

A **palette** = a coordinated set of surface assets per role:

| role | used for | override control |
|---|---|---|
| Field | main paving | the type's existing field control (plaza: `surface`) |
| Border | frame band along the outline, kerbs, the ring round the pad | `borderSurface` |
| Focal | the pad round the focal point (rondel) | `focalSurface` |
| Path | walks from the entrances | `pathSurface` |
| Inlay | thin dark accents: inlay lines, tree pits, an outer ring | `inlaySurface` |

Palettes (ids are save format; tones = mean colour of the mods' own icon images, see GroundSurfaces):

| id | label | field · border · focal · path · inlay |
|---|---|---|
| classic | Classic stone (calm default) | EAI Grey Paving Stone · G87 Natural Stone 08 (slate) · G87 Natural Stone 01 (grey fan) · EAI Herringbone 001 · G87 Natural Stone 04 |
| sandstone | Warm sandstone | Road Textures Concrete Stripped Beige · G87 Clay Paver M · G87 Natural Stone 07 (pale flags) · Natural Stone 03 · Clay Paver C |
| brickStone | Brick & stone | G87 Clay Paver A (muted brick) · Natural Stone 02 (granite setts) · Paving Slab A Grey · Natural Stone 02 · Clay Paver F |
| modern | Modern concrete | G87 Regular Blocks 13 · Paving Slab B Black · EAI Polished Concrete · Regular Blocks 12 · EAI Hexagonal Dark |
| terracotta | Terracotta (the old "warm" look — an option, never a default) | Paving Slab A Beige · Clay Paver D · Paving Slab A White · Slab A White · Clay Paver F |
| cobble | Cobbled old town | Natural Stone 02 · Natural Stone 08 · Natural Stone 01 · Natural Stone 07 · Natural Stone 04 |
| garden | Garden brick | Clay Paver C · Natural Stone 03 · Natural Stone 07 · Natural Stone 07 · Clay Paver M |

Every role has fallbacks ending in the vanilla surface that reads closest (Tiles 01/02/03, Concrete 01/02).

**Control** (`GroundPalettes.DefineControls`): a `palette` choice first in the ground section — Automatic, the seven
palettes, Custom — with shared labels (`Placemaker.Palette.*`, Lang/en-US.palettes.json) and a one-line description
per option ("Pale granite slabs · slate border · grey fan-set rondel · herringbone walks") shown as the option's
tooltip, since the panel has no swatches yet. `GroundPalettes.Swatch(p)` already returns role → tone for s16's
`OptionSwatch` stripes.

**Automatic** = the type's own palette: plaza presets each declare one (civic/amphitheatre → classic, fountain/piazza
→ sandstone, market → cobble, garden/sunken/green → garden, cafe → brick & stone, level presets → modern); without a
preset the square's character decides, calm stone by default.

**Saves**: control ids are unchanged (`surface` = field; the old `paveStyle` control stays, hidden). An old save with
`paveStyle` and no palette keeps its look (warm → terracotta, civic → classic …) while showing Automatic; picking a
palette wins.

**Custom**: `GroundPalettes.IsCustom / Displayed / ClearOverrides` implement the road-mix semantics (any role
override → "Custom"; picking a palette clears the overrides). **Lead, contract:** wiring needs s16's
`ILinkedControls` (Contracts.cs, DesignEditor, UiModels — not on master yet). Once merged, PlazaLayer adds
`ILinkedControls` with `OnValueSet: controlId == "palette" → GroundPalettes.ClearOverrides(...)` and
`Derive: settings["palette"] = GroundPalettes.Displayed(settings, "surface")` (keep the chosen palette when no role is
overridden). Until then a role pick simply overrides that role of the chosen palette, and "Custom" behaves as
Automatic. Also set `OptionSchema.DetailKey = GroundPalettes.DetailKey(id)` and `Swatch` from `GroundPalettes.Swatch`
on the palette options so the description is visible without hovering.

## 3. Types that should adopt palettes next

1. **Market square** (`Leisure/MarketSquareLayer.cs`) — hard-codes Warm/Cobble: add `GroundPalettes.DefineControls`,
   Automatic = cobble (fountain kind → sandstone instead of the ochre "warm").
2. **Transit plaza** (`PavingPalettes.Transit`) — Automatic = modern.
3. **Amphitheatre** floor/orchestra (`PavingPalettes.Warm.Field/Accent` = the mustard slab) — Automatic = sandstone.
4. **Fairground** (Warm / Cobble) — Automatic = cobble.
5. **Park plaza place and paved paths** (park's plaza feature, path surfaces) — palette per park style (formal →
   classic, romantic → garden).
6. **Commercial** plazas (shopping plaza, outdoor mall) — modern / brick & stone.

Each adoption = `GroundPalettes.DefineControls(c, "ground", "<fieldControl>")` + `GroundPalettes.Resolve(ctx.Settings,
"<auto id>", "<fieldControl>")` and laying roles through `PavingPlan` (pieces never overlap).

## Follow-up (after merging s16 into s18)

* **Linked like a road mix** — PlazaLayer, MarketSquareLayer, TransitPlazaLayer, AmphitheatreLayer and FairgroundLayer
  implement `ILinkedControls` via `GroundPalettes.OnValueSet / Derive`: picking a palette clears the role picks and
  records it in the hidden `paletteBase`; any role pick turns the chooser to Custom, which keeps the stones of the
  palette it started from; clearing the last pick returns to that palette.
* **Option cards**: each palette option has `DetailKey` (the visible description line) and a five-stripe `Swatch`
  (roles field/border/focal/path/inlay, measured tones). The UI draws it as `PaletteSwatch` (a tiny square: field framed
  by the band, round focal pad, a walk, inlay corners); road mixes keep `RoadSwatch`. **PROTOCOL 17** (C# + contract.ts)
  for the new swatch roles.
* **Adopted**: market square (Automatic: cobble, sandstone round a fountain), transit plaza (modern; forecourt →
  classic), amphitheatre rows/orchestra (sandstone; cinema → modern), fairground pads and paved site (cobble at
  Christmas, else sandstone; new field control `pavingSurface`). None defaults to the ochre "warm" palette any more.
* UI test: `UI/mock/tests/palettes.spec.ts` (real Core) — palette cards with swatches, picking one.
