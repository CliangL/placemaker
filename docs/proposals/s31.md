# s31 — rough ground: paths level themselves, pads are flat, the Terrain feature behaves

Playtest (4 screenshots, a park on an already bumpy hill with the **Terrain** feature — "bumpiness / variation: mounds,
hills" — switched on): paved paths and a green bike path warped and folded, grass tore up *through* the paving in jagged
shapes, white "curtain" seams where path edges met ground at another height, a bike path curled over a bump and was chopped
into angular pieces where it joined a paved path, the playground mulch draped over a lumpy slope, picnic tables, a BBQ grill
and play equipment stood tilted, benches on a hump. User: *"Everything should flatten out where it needs to be flat. Or be
terraced. Roads/paths should be like roads in the game when you place them: they just level themselves."*

## Causes (reproduced: `SiteHarmonyTests.WithoutHarmony_TheOldArtefactsAreBack`, gallery `--no-harmony`)

| Artefact | Cause |
|---|---|
| **Curtains, grass tearing through the paving** | The Terrain feature is a child step (order 140) of the park: it ran **after** the park had laid its paths and shaped **12 m LEVEL cells** (`TerrainShaper`) straight under them — its `Skip` only knew reserved *polygons* (places), not path corridors or furniture. The build's raster (`TerraformRaster`) banks only *outside* the union of rings, so two neighbouring level cells at different heights are a vertical step of up to max-slope × 12 m (20 % → 2.4 m). In CS2 that is a terrain cliff (the white/grey face) cutting through whatever lies on it. Any layer emitting level cells next to another's patches did the same (later layer wins, no blend). |
| **Paths warped and folded, bike path curled over a bump** | Nothing graded paths. A terrain-following net is split by `CourseFitting` wherever it leaves the ground by 0.35 m (pieces ≥ 6 m) and every node is pinned to the ground, so the path follows every lump. Measured on the rough test site: **along-grade 22 % (2.7× a walk's limit)**. |
| **White seams at path edges, torn wedges** | CS2 levels a net across its width per piece. On a cross-slope each piece cut one side and filled the other — **cross-slope up to 17–26 %** under the paths — and the terrain between pieces/beside them met the levelled strip at another height. |
| **Bike path chopped into pieces at the junction** | Junction pieces were pinned to the (bumpy) ground independently: the two paths met at different heights and their level strips overlapped at different levels. |
| **Mulch draped over the lumps, tables/grill/play equipment tilted** | Surfaces (the playground's mulch is a `Decorative` surface, paving links, picnic pads) were placed on the raw ground; furniture had no pads. **9–13 of 41 furniture items on > 12 %**. |
| **Objects floating / buried (red ticks in the gallery 3D view)** | The park planned its objects on the ground *before* its Terrain child shaped it; nothing re-snapped them: **objects up to 2 m off** the built ground. |

Before / after on the gallery's rough site (1.2 ha, ±2 m swells + ±0.6 m bumps), Terrain feature "rolling":

## The fix: one "site harmony" pass (`Core/Build/SiteHarmony.cs`)

Runs once in `Composer.Compose` after every layer, `PathJoins`, dressing and cutouts — **before** `SiteSafety` (which still
cuts the field around building lots) and `ZoneGuard`. Pure, deterministic, cancellable; returns quietly on a level, even site
whose layers reshape nothing (the common flat case costs nothing).

**Who takes part.** Every layer except those whose ground is their own design or another pass's job (`OwnGround`): water
(beach, waterfront, shore, wetland, pond — s30), parking and restArea lot grading (s25/s29), roads, commercial grading
(strip mall, shopping plaza, outdoor mall, shops park), landforms (lookout hill, golf, quarry, landfill, amphitheatre,
skate bowl, dirt jumps), rough-by-design yards (construction site, abandoned lot, scrapyard, logistics yard, urban ruins,
hiking trail). Their terraform (plus its 4 m bank), nets (+3 m), surfaces/areas (+2 m) and props (2.5 m) are **locked**;
so are decks/bridges of participating layers and every water terraform. The free zone is the union of the participating
fill layers' outlines (`ComposedPlan.FillRings`, new).

**The field.** A 2 m node grid (grows past 120 k nodes). Targets are composed in priority order — the request's
*paths > pads > surfaces > decorative mounds > original*:

1. **Intended ground.** The build's own raster (1.75 m, 4 m bank) of the kept terraform (K), of the owners' grading (N) and
   of everything (A). The Terrain feature's change (D = A − N) is smoothed and **masked**: zero within half width + 1 m + 3 m
   of every path (fading over 8 m), 3 m around every pad surface (8 m fade), 2 m around furniture (6 m fade). *Mounds never
   cross paths.*
2. **Surfaces → pads** (`IsPadSurface`: paving, plaza, asphalt, ground, and feature-sized decorative ones ≤ 2500 m² such as
   play mulch; never grass, water, crops). Touching surfaces of one kind are **one pad** (a plaza's pieces get one plane).
   Smallest first, the larger owns overlaps. Least-squares plane, tilt ≤ 2 % (≤ 600 m²) / 3 %, ≤ 10 % at the outline (it
   meets the street as it is); a 3 m level margin (the raster's texels reach that far); a bank ≤ 1 : 4 that gives way to
   ground already protected. Large (> 2000 m²) or long (> 45 m) surfaces are **smoothed** instead (≈ 10 m low-pass: the
   bumps go, the hill stays — one plane over a 100 m square on a hill would be metres of cut). A surface the owner already
   graded (≥ half its nodes changed by the owner's terraform) keeps it.
3. **Paths** (every terrain-following non-fence net of a participating layer, bike/trail by category). One graph: vertices
   every ~2 m, shared ends are one vertex (junctions share one height), **X crossings without a node are tied**, walks whose
   level bands touch are soft-tied (smoothed towards each other). Profile = Laplacian smoothing along the graph with a weak
   pull to the ground (≈ 14 m, like a vanilla net), then the grade limit — walks/wide walks/drives **8 %**
   (`TerrainSurvey.WalkGrade`), bike **6 %**, trails 15 % — then the earthwork cap (±3.5 m). Pinned: ends at the outline
   (the street's ground), ends on decks/bridges (their height), vertices on a large graded surface (≥ 300 m²: the plaza is
   the ground there). Then a **corridor**: level ACROSS the path out to half width + 1 m shoulder + ¾ node (the "level
   band"), bank ≤ 1 : 4 back to the ground beyond (giving way to graded surfaces); near junctions (≤ 9 m) the two nearest
   paths' heights blend over 3 m. Surfaces a path runs into are refitted *with* the band (≤ 150 m² — beds, link strips —
   take one plane through the band heights; bigger ones a ≤ 10 % plane), so they meet the walk without a crease. Edges still
   over the limit (pinned ends, earthwork cap) → Info `Placemaker.Harmony.Issue.SteepPath`.
4. **Furniture pads** (`IsFurniture`: benches, tables, grills, play equipment, kiosks, centrepieces, bins; not hedges,
   bollards, stepping stones, planters, fences, graves — they follow the ground). Items whose level discs would touch are one
   cluster: one level when the cluster spans ≤ 0.8 m, else **one terrace plane** (≤ 5 %, else ≤ 10 %) when that stays within
   1.5 m, else each item on its own small pad. A cluster touching a path's level band continues that band. Flat r + 1.75 m,
   bank ≤ 1 : 4 that gives way to earlier pads; never inside a level band or a graded surface (the surface is the pad). Spots
   already level are still protected from the next step.
5. **One smooth change.** The change O = T − K is Laplace-smoothed (20 passes) **where nothing is protected** — this melts
   the owners' level-cell steps (curtains) without touching bands, pads and planes. What the pass adds over the owners'
   grading dies out towards the outline / locked ground no steeper than **1 : 3** and stays within ±3.5 m.
6. **Emit once.** The participating layers' terraform (except water, and patches reaching outside the free zone) is
   removed and the field is emitted as **one grid of sloped quads** (`PlannedTerraform.Sloped`, clipped to the outline) on the
   first participating fill layer (the park). Cells change ≥ 0.12 m, or ≥ 2 cm under bands/pads/surfaces (2 % across a 3 m
   path is 6 cm). The permanent-terrain warning is added when anything changes.
7. **Re-snap.** Objects, surface/area rings and terrain-following nets (ends on the ground, inner controls by
   `CourseFitting.FitProfile`) of the participating layers go onto the final ground — the build's own raster — when they were
   on the ground before (within 1.5 m of the original or any planned ground); decks and things on locked ground keep theirs.
   The build's `GroundHeight` reads the same raster, so its course fitting now finds a smooth, level-across ground and splits
   paths only where the profile really bends.

Facts on the host plan: `harmonyCells, harmonyPaths, harmonySurfaces, harmonyPads, harmonyMaxCut, harmonyMaxFill,
harmonySteepEdges, harmonyDecorInCorridor (0 by construction), harmonyDecorMasked`.

## The Terrain feature (`Core/Layers/AddOns/TerrainAddOn.cs`, `Core/Layers/Common/TerrainShaper.cs`)

* **Smooth.** New `TerrainSpec.Sloped`: the same shapes evaluated on a ≥ 4 m node grid (`Common/Sculpt`), grade-limited
  (max slope), capped towards the outline, emitted as sloped quads. Mounds and hills are continuous landforms; terraces,
  sunken/raised and the bowl get ramps at the max slope instead of cliffs. Other `TerrainShaper` users are unchanged (their
  level cells are melted by step 5 where they take part).
* **Keeps off what people use.** New `TerrainSpec.Weight`: for mounds / hills / berms the change fades to zero within half
  width + 3 m of the parent's path corridors (8 m fade) and 1.5 m of non-tree discs (5 m fade).
* **Scales back on rough ground.** `Roughness` = RMS of the ground about its ±8 m box mean (a steady slope is not rough).
  From 0.25 m the height scales down linearly; at ≥ 0.65 m (scale < 0.2) nothing is added. Info
  `Placemaker.AddOn.Terrain.Issue.ScaledBack` / `RoughGround`; fact `terrainRoughness`.

## Metrics (`SiteHarmony.Measure`) and tests (`Tests/Placemaker.Tests/SiteHarmonyTests.cs`)

`Measure(plan, site)` judges the ground the build leaves (the real raster over the final plan) inside the participating
outlines: **MaxJump** — the largest step of the *change* (final − original) between neighbouring 1.75 m cells, more than 3 m
inside and 4 m from locked ground (a curtain); **MaxPathCross / P95** — slope between a path's two edges (junctions and
crossings ± half width + 5 m excepted); **MaxPathGrade / Ratio** — along-grade over 4 m against the path's limit (beyond 6 m
of the outline); **MaxPadDeviation / MaxPadTilt** — pads grouped like the pass, judged 1 m inside, off the level bands, not at
the outline, not large/long; **MaxObjectOff**; **MaxFurnitureGrade / FurnitureSteep** (> 12 %, beyond 3 m of the outline).

Test sites: 150 × 110 m with streets all round; `rough` (±2 m swells at 24 m + ±0.6 m bumps at 9 m), `hill` (the same
noise on a 5 m hill and 2 % tilt), `smoothhill` (no bumps). Tests:
* every participating park/leisure type (22) on `hill` with the Terrain feature (rolling hills): no errors, jump ≤ 0.8 m,
  cross ≤ 2 %, along-grade ≤ 1.1 × limit (or the SteepPath issue), pads within ±0.15 m and ≤ 3.5 %, objects within 0.3 m of
  the final ground, furniture on > 12 % at most 1 (or 5 %), decoration inside corridors 0;
* park, picnic grounds, adventure playground, sports park, campground, botanical garden, plaza on `rough`, terrain on/off;
* the old artefacts come back without the pass (cross > 10 %, grade > 1.3×, > 3 furniture on slopes);
* mounds never inside path corridors (park, picnic grounds, botanical garden: ground under the paths moves ≤ 0.1 m when the
  Terrain feature goes on, while the lawns change > 0.5 m);
* one field (single owner, all sloped); flat ground without the feature → no terraform, no warning; roughness scale-back;
  the feature alone is sloped and keeps 0.15 m off path ground; mixed recipes (roads + park blocks, split park | plaza);
  5 ha park on the hill < 4 s (measured ≈ 0.3–0.6 s).
* `Sweep_AllTypes_Print`, `Details_ForTypes` (`HARMONY_TYPES=a,b`, `HARMONY_AT=x:z`), `Profile_FiveHectares`
  (`HARMONY_PROFILE=type`) are debug aids that print and assert nothing.
* `OrchardParkTests.TerracedOliveGroveIsBlendedAtTheEdge` now checks the merged field (the terrace change lives on the orchard
  plan).

## Coordination (s29 parking grading, s30 waterfront / shore)

Their layers are in `OwnGround` and are only *locked*: harmony never changes their terraform, nets, surfaces or props, and
keeps 2–4 m off them. A park feature on the shore (`shore`) or a pond stays theirs. If s29/s30 want their walks graded the
same way they can call into the helpers (`FitPlane`, `IsPadSurface`, `IsFurniture`, `Measure` for their tests) or drop
their id from `OwnGround`. Nothing in `Layers/Types/Waterfront|Beach` or `Layers/Parking` was touched.

## Needs in-game verification

* Paths over a graded corridor: CS2's own net terrain clip on top of an already level-across band should leave no seam; the
  build's terraform tolerance is 0.3 m per texel (2 % of cells may be off) — watch for small ripples under paths.
* Number of terraform quads (≈ 2–4 k for 1.5 ha, ≈ 10 k for 4 ha on rough ground): one raster, a handful of brush
  dispatches as before, but check build time and the receipt size on a 10 ha park.
* The playground mulch / picnic pads / benches standing level; a cluster terraced as one gentle plane on a slope.
* The Terrain feature on rough ground: Info "made lower" / "no extra mounds"; mounds never under paths.
* Log: `Placemaker terraform: N items, raster …` then `reached its target`.

## Not done / proposals

* Plazas on steep ground are smoothed, not terraced: an automatic "levels" choice (terraces with steps) when the slope under
  a plaza exceeds ~4 % would match "or be terraced".
* Emit merged quads (rows of cells with one bilinear patch each) to cut the item count on big sites.
* A visible control ("Grade paths and pads", default on) if players want the old draped look.
* Wetland / shore kit / hiking trails could use the same path profile + corridor for their walks (s30 / trails owners).
