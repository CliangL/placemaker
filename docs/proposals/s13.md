# s13 — texture paint as a plan primitive; beach art pass

Research: RESEARCH-NOTES-ADDENDUM "Terrain texture painting" (rules 57–62).

## Contract changes (lead, please review)

- `Core/Contracts.cs` `PlannedPaint`: appended optional fields `Feather`, `Noise`, `NoiseScale`, `Seed`, `GradientFrom`,
  `GradientFull`, `GradientFade`, `GradientMin`. Defaults reproduce the old behaviour. Not persisted anywhere.
- New `TerrainMaterials` constants (Erase 0, Dry 1, Lush 2, Dirt 3, Sand 4 — the vanilla slot contents).
- `TerrainPaint.Patch` keeps emitting two paints (counts unchanged) but the outline paint now feathers inside the ring with
  a little noise instead of a ±1.5 m edge; new `TerrainPaint.Field(...)` for one feathered/gradient field.
- `Shoreline.Foreshore(reach, intoWater)` / `SignedDepth`; `ShoreTerrain.Shape(..., beyond)` shapes the foreshore too.

## Fix the paint layer defaults of other types (high value, cheap)

Most types pick "1" for dirt/mud/worn ground — on vanilla maps that is **Dry grass** (the "grey" look). Suggested defaults:

| type / control | now | suggested |
|---|---|---|
| ConstructionSite.paintLayer (mud), SpoilHeaps.paintLayer, Quarry.paintLayer (pit), Landfill, Scrapyard, AbandonedLot, DirtJumps, UrbanFarm.soilLayer | 1 Dry | 3 Dirt |
| Park.desireLayer (worn paths), AdventurePlayground.paintLayer (worn ground), Fairground.paintLayer | 1 Dry | 3 Dirt at 0.5–0.7 strength |
| GroundPatches.dirtLayer / gravelLayer | 1 / 1 | 3 / 3 (weaker) |
| GroundPatches.rockyLayer, Rocks.patchLayer | 3 / 1 | 3 Dirt + rocks (no rock material exists) |
| Farmstead.paintLayer (plough), stripeLayer | 2 Lush / 3 | 3 Dirt / 2 Lush |
| OrchardPark meadow / aisle | 1 / 2 | 2 Lush / 1 Dry |
| Golf.roughLayer, NatureReserve.floorLayer, Park.naturalLayer, HikingTrail, LookoutHill | 1 Dry | 1 Dry is right (meadow/rough) |
| Wetland.marshLayer | 1 | 2 Lush |
| ZenGarden gravel / rake / moss | 2 / 3 / 1 | 4 Sand (raked gravel reads as sand) / 3 / 2 Lush |

Also rename the "Terrain layer" labels to say what the numbers mean (beach tooltip does: "1 dry grass, 2 lush grass,
3 dirt, 4 sand"); a Choice control would be nicer but would change the control kind (save format) — add a new id instead.

## Types that should adopt texture fields next (one line each)

- **Construction site / quarry / landfill / scrapyard**: one Dirt field over the working area, feather 4 m, noise 2 m; haul
  roads as Dirt strokes (ring = buffered polyline) at 0.8.
- **Farm tracks / farmstead yard**: Dirt strokes along tracks (full in the wheel ruts, fade 2 m), yard Dirt fading into Lush.
- **Playgrounds / picnic areas / campground pitches**: worn ground = Dry→Dirt field, full under the equipment, `GradientFrom`
  = the play items' centres (points), fade 6 m.
- **Rocky outcrops** (RocksAddOn): Dirt field with high noise (3 m) under each cluster, gradient from the cluster centre.
- **Path edges in parks**: a 1–1.5 m Dry stroke along gravel/dirt paths at 0.4 (trampled verge).
- **Sports infields** (baseball diamond, running track infield): Dirt field with a 1 m feather — crisp but not a quad.
- **Wetland / lakeshore**: Lush band along the water (gradient from the shore, fade 8 m) + Dirt mudflats near the reeds.
- **Dunes / lookout hill summits**: Dry on crests (gradient from the ridge line).

## Generic "soft edge blend" for every build — not implemented

Considered: paint a feathered band along every build's outline to blend it into its surroundings. Left out because there
is no single right material (a plaza wants nothing, a park wants Lush, a yard Dirt), it paints outside what the player
drew, and it is permanent on every build. If wanted later: an off-by-default recipe-level option
`edgeBlend { material, width }` handled once in the Composer (one `TerrainPaint.Field` per root region, ring = outline
buffered by `width`, gradient from the outline, fade `width`) — about 30 lines plus a control; no game changes needed.

## Beach (done here)

Sand = one Sand field from the real water (Foreshore, up to 24 m past the outline, 8 m into the water) to the back of
the beach, fading over `sandBlend` m; dunes a lighter field thinning up the slope; rocky = Dirt + Sand pockets. Sand
surface is opt-in. Accesses are short and end on landings (wood deck pack → vanilla tiles). Lifeguard posts = umpire chair
+ lifebuoy + flag (no vanilla tower). One umbrella and one lounger model per build, loose rows with aisles. Waterfront got
`shoreTexture` (auto: sand for promenade/pier, dirt for fishing).

Needs in-game verification: stamp orientation (gradient on the water side), sand actually covering the procedural rock on a
steep bank, foreshore carve with real water (never > 0.6 m below the surface), landing surface ids, umpire chair as
lifeguard post, performance of one stamp per frame on a 5 ha beach.
