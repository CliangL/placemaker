# s12 — Street shops, much more advanced

User: "no street shops or a lot of street shops, or facing the other way with a little alleyway going around, or
alleyways going through with street shops inside, or speckled street shops. And the park would interweave itself
between the street shops. And it should be on by default in the ones that make sense."

## What changed

`Core/Layers/Common/StreetShops.cs` (new) plans the shop land; `LayerBase` (CarveStreetShops / FinishStreetShops)
hosts it for every type with `HostsStreetShops`. The host plans on its outline **minus** the shop land and alleys,
so its paths, trees, entrances and lawns fill the gaps between the shops. The `edgeZoning` feature zones each
`shops` region from the edges that face a street or a planned alley.

Controls (under the "Street shops" feature toggle; ids are save format):

| id | values | note |
|---|---|---|
| `shopAmount` (new) | none · few · some · lots | none = no shops even with the feature on; missing in old saves → type default |
| `shopLayout` | band (Row) · pads (Speckled) · clusters · inward (Facing in) · lane (Alley) | `band`/`pads` keep their ids; unknown values plan as Row |
| `shopDepth` | 8–48 m, whole cells | unchanged |

Labels/tooltips are shared keys `Placemaker.Shops.*` (en-US.leisure.json); the per-type `Placemaker.<Area>.shopLayout/shopDepth`
keys were removed (nothing reads them any more).

### Layouts

- **Row** — continuous shops along every street frontage, gaps at the entrances; amount = share of each frontage piece
  (40 / 70 / 100 %), centred. At a street corner the second frontage is shortened by whole cells (the row turns as an L).
- **Speckled** — small pads (1–3 cells wide, 1–3 deep) at random intervals; lawn between pads reaches the street.
  Amount = gap range (36–64 / 14–30 / 8–18 m).
- **Clusters** — one square corner block at each street corner, then 32–40 m blocks of uneven (stepped) depth with
  green fingers between them; "few" = one 24 m cluster on each of the longest third of the frontage pieces.
- **Facing in** — per frontage piece a U-shaped alley (vanilla `Alley`, fallback `Small Road`): both legs join the
  street (attached ends, like parking alleys), 12 m clear of street corners; the shops sit between the street and the
  alley's back run, the place lies beyond the alley. Few = the longest piece only; lots = every piece ≥ 40 m.
- **Alley** — a dead-end lane from the street into the area (35 / 50 / 65 % of the way across), shops on both sides
  (≤ 2 cells deep). Its dead end becomes an extra entrance for the host (only when the player placed entrances), and
  the host's path end is snapped onto the lane's end node, so the lane carries on as a park path. Lots = up to two lanes.

Both alley layouts need an **existing** street to join (a street the parent layer plans cannot take a mid-segment
junction); without one they fall back to Row with Info `Placemaker.Feature.streetShops.Issue.NoAlley`.

Safety rules: shop pieces are whole 8 m cells, inside the outline, never overlapping each other, never in water
(`SiteContext.WaterDepthAt`; frontage running into a lake is cut). Shop regions are tagged `place:shops` too, so the
composer reserves them for the host's other features (big trees, picnic… plan on the whole outline); the zoning
feature filling a region still sees it free. Host objects whose footprint reaches onto shop land are dropped.

### Small shared changes (please review, lead)

- `EdgeZoningAddOn`: `NetRole.Alley` corridors count as roads (shops face the planned alleys); a reserved disc only
  blocks a zone quad when it reaches more than half its radius into it (a tree crown brushing the edge used to drop
  a whole cell row); reserved polygons are tested against a 0.3 m inset quad (the neighbouring shop region sharing an
  edge no longer counts as "occupied").
- `StreetFrontage`: the old `Bands`/`Carve` were removed (StreetShops replaces them).
- Gallery: zoning quads are drawn (commercial blue) with shop regions outlined; `--shops` renders every layout/amount
  combination for each host type. Fixture `catalog-bounds.json` gained `"Alley":{"w":8}` (vanilla, 8 m) so the gallery
  draws alleys at their real width (RefreshFixture keeps existing items).

## Open problems / engine questions

1. **Which way the shops face is the game's choice.** Shop cells between the street and a facing-in alley lie in the
   zone blocks of both nets; CS2 resolves overlapping blocks itself, so buildings may face the street instead of the
   alley. The zoning is valid either way; verify in game and, if needed, leave a 1-cell green strip on the street side.
2. **Vanilla `Alley` must carry zone blocks.** PLT built its zoning road from it and its parking-connector clone
   explicitly nulled `ZoneBlock`, which says the vanilla one has them; not yet verified in game.
3. Alley legs join existing streets 12 m from street corners at minimum; very short frontage pieces (< 40 m) get no unit.
4. A plaza with lanes recentres its rondel on the largest remaining piece (it plans on the carved outline).
