# s33 — terrain paint: material census, paint toolkit, ground paint everywhere, painted lawns

User: *"TERRAIN PAINT is what gives us power: our own custom path-looking things, worn path edges done correctly … paint a
path, half-texture a whole surface, paint the whole thing, blend between two things, gradients, path gradients, sphere
gradients. Draw dark mulch under each tree."* Approved: naturalistic types leave the lawn as bare terrain + paint; the grass
surface stays for formal / memorial / cemetery types and as an option. Full write-up: **docs/TERRAIN-PAINT.md**; engine
rules RESEARCH-NOTES-ADDENDUM 86–89.

## Findings that change earlier assumptions

- The splatmap keeps **one 4-bit weight per material** (kernel disassembled). Materials are independent; paint is a max;
  repainting is a no-op; < 1/30 vanishes. "Later layer wins" and "painting twice thickens" (s13) were wrong.
- A material tool at strength 0 **erases that material only**. Brush orientation is identity (no calibration needed).
- Vanilla "Lush" is **Forest_temperate** — a dark forest floor, the darkest material, not a fresh lawn. Sand is grey-beige.
  The procedural base grass IS the lawn; paint varies it.
- Only one terrain render settings prefab ships; custom maps can use any texture per slot; mods cannot add materials.

- Controls (dressing ids, so type control counts are unchanged): section **Ground paint** (`groundPaint`): `dressLawn`

  `--map arid|mediterranean|tropic|alpine|shuffled`. Invisible paths drawn as a dotted line.

## Lawn default changes

Painted terrain: park (all lived-in/gritty presets), picnicGrounds, dogPark, greenwayPark, adventurePlayground, animalPark,
communityGarden, orchardPark. Grass surface: park formal/garden/victorian/modern, formalGarden, botanicalGarden,
cemeteryGrounds, memorialPark, fairground, sportsPark, amphitheatre, skatePark, urbanFarm.

## Not done / next

- In-game verification: the weights' look at the chosen strengths (gallery is an approximation of the terrain shader:
  sequential lerp of the four weights), the per-material erase (kernel reading says yes), palette capture on a custom map.
- Campground has its own `groundSurface` (bare by default) but no lawn choice, so no lawn variation yet (mulch / pitch wear yes).
- Pitch wear only shows on bare pitches; turf pitches are grass surfaces (wear masked). A "painted turf" pitch option would
  make worn goalmouths visible.
- A desire line planned by the park layer may cross a later surface (plaza / playground pad): invisible there, but counted.
- Types' own paint (beach sand, construction mud, farm soil…) still uses ring paints; moving them to the toolkit (strokes
  for haul roads and tracks, blends for beach → dune) is mechanical.
- s30 (waterfront) and s32 (malls) files untouched; their lawns (`WaterfrontLayer`, `Mall.cs`) still lay grass surfaces.
