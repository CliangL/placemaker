# s25 — parking lots and commercial sites that work in game

Playtest (screenshots 41–43, Placemaker.log 13:44–13:46):

* **Strip mall on a slope (41):** the lot was a blank pale slab (no bays, aisles or lamps), dark decal blobs all over it,
  white mounds/cones where the lanes meet the lot and at its corners. Across the lane the shop plots were pits with
  retaining walls, dirt floors and white fences on three sides.
* **Parking type (42/43):** the Design preview drew every stall; the built twin beside it had no stall lines at all
  (a few accessible hatches and EV decals survived), opaque oil/stain blobs everywhere, pale concrete, the lot
  following every lump of the ground. User: "I don't see the parking locations. Too many decals … It's not flat —
  shouldn't each individual place be flat, and going up/down to the next level terrace?"

## Root causes

| Artefact | Cause |
|---|---|
| **No stall lines after the build** | The build was an *instant build from the ghost* (log: `ghost 71: 1823/1823 parts, 0 rejected, 1095 optional objects dropped (collisions)` → `instant build from the ghost` → `tagged 796`). The ghost emits every object with `CreationFlags.Optional`; an optional temporary overlapping another optional one is cancelled **together with it** (ValidationSystem CancelOptional, ADDENDUM 51). `ParkingLotDecal01` boxes are 3.2 m wide on 3.0 m stalls, so every regular stall overlapped its neighbours and whole rows cancelled; the 5 m accessible decals (exactly one stall wide) and a few EV pairs survived. The instant build adopts the ghost's temporaries as they are. Secondary: the collision retry (`ExcludeErroneousItems`) maps errors to plan items **by position (2 m)**, so an errored island/lamp/surface could take the stall markings next to it with it. |
| **Too many, too prominent decals** | s21 Parking dressing rates (oil in 42 % of bays plus a drip, tyre marks at 8 % of mouths, stains every 7 m of aisle, fans at every turn) **plus** the Paved scatter (cracks/stains/patches) also dressing the lot asphalt **plus** the commercial types' default *Decals* feature (cracks theme, 0.4) on all paved ground. |
| **Pale concrete lot** | Default lot surface "EAI … Concrete Asphalt 001" reads as a light slab under CS2 lighting (and without stall lines nothing broke it up). |
| **Lot not flat on slopes** | The Parking planner never graded: every bay, aisle and lamp sat on the raw terrain. |
| **White cones at lane/lot junctions and corners** | A CS2 road is level across. The commercial lanes were laid on the raw cross-slope; the game cuts one side, fills the other and banks the ground beside the lane — into the lot, whose light surface drapes over the banks (cones at bends and junctions). The old "Flatten terrain" feature (one plane over the whole site, 12 m level cells) made it worse when switched on. |
| **Pits with walls and white fences on the plots** | Not Placemaker terraform or fences: **vanilla construction sites**. A zoned building levels its lot to the height of the road in front; on ground rising away from the lane it digs a pit with retaining walls (dirt floor = construction ground, white fences = construction fences). The plots were simply not at their road's grade. (No fence feature, region border fence or plot terraform exists in the Commercial plans — now tested.) |

## Fixes

**Stall markings always built** (`Core/Build/PlanWork.cs`, `Game/Build/BuildService.Emit.cs`, `BuildService.Adopt.cs`, `BuildService.cs`)
* `PlanWork.IsFunctional(role)`: ParkingBay, Charger, Arrow. The ghost emits them **non-optional** (as ParkingLotTool builds
  them) — overlapping optional stalls can no longer cancel each other, so the instant build keeps them.
* `PlanWork.Excludable(plan, item)`: the collision retry never leaves a functional piece out because something *near* it
  failed (probe match); one that is itself rejected (exact match) only goes after two retries did not settle it.
* Build log: `Placemaker parking: N of M stall markings built` (warning when N < M; counts markings kept despite errors).
* Not done (deliberately): sinking stall decals to elevation −0.01 like s21's decorative decals — the stall decal owns the
  `ParkingLane` sub-lane; an Underground collision mask on its owner is unverified for lane connection. Revisit only if the
  log line shows missing markings after this fix.

**Calm lots** (`Core/Decals/DressingRules.cs`, `Core/Layers/Commercial/CommercialLayer.cs`)
* Parking dressing: oil in `OilShare` = 8 % of bays (one blot, no drip), tyre marks 1.5 %, repairs/litter/glass ~1–3 %,
  aisle stains 2.5 % per 7 m, entrance fans 30 %, none at plain turns. Amount sliders / gritty style still add more.
* Lot asphalt is dressed by the Parking family only (the Paved scatter skips `SurfaceRole.Asphalt`).
* Commercial types: the *Decals* feature is off by default; the *Flatten terrain* feature is removed from the list (the
  type grades itself; saved designs that had it still run it).

**Dark asphalt** (`Core/Layers/Parking/ParkingAssets.cs`): default `G87 Vanilla Asphalt Pavement G87 VA Surface GND Surface`
(the road asphalt as a surface), then EAI Concrete Asphalt, then vanilla Pavement. *Needs in-game confirmation of the look.*

**Lot grading** (`Core/Layers/Parking/LotGrading.cs`, `PlanBuilder.cs`, new control `grading`, default on)
* Tiers = the stall rows along x with the aisle they face (rows keyed by `Bay.Row`, so a perimeter line serving four sides
  does not merge everything); one plane per tier: level front-to-back within 2 %, one shared cross-fall along the rows ≤ 2 %.
* Steps between tiers are chained from the first tier: the fitted step clamped to a planted embankment ≤ 1 : 2.5 over the
  gap **and** to an 8 % ramp of the perimeter road between tier middles; the chain then shifts so cut and fill balance.
* Beyond the row ends (perimeter road, side rows) the ground ramps linearly from tier middle to tier middle, blended over
  4 m starting 2 m past the last stall. Cross lanes over a terrace edge steeper than 12 % are dropped (the gap stays paved).
* Emitted as a few dozen **sloped** polygons (cells between the tier/ramp breaks, clipped to the outline incl. the setback
  strip) — planes are exact under the build's mean-value raster; no 4 m staircase. Ground already within 0.15 m of the
  graded surface → nothing emitted (flat sites untouched). Everything (bays, aisles, lamps, plants, surfaces) is planned on
  the graded ground (`AreaPlan.HeightAfter`). Facts `gradeTiers/MaxCut/MaxFill/RowGrade/MaxStep`; > 2.5 m earthwork → Info
  `Placemaker.Parking.Issue.SteepGrade`.

**Commercial site at grade** (`Core/Layers/Commercial/MallGrading.cs`, `Mall.Finish`)
* One Sculpt (2 m nodes) over the site after the sub-plans; the lots' own grading is fixed ground (registered in
  `ctx.Ground`).
* Lanes: a smooth longitudinal profile (≤ 8 %), pinned to the street at attached ends, raw samples taken from the graded
  lot / street-facing plot beside them; level across the lane plus a 2 m shoulder.
* Shop plots: level at the height of the road they face, along the frontage (existing streets first: where a plot lies
  between a street and a lane it faces the street). Margins (one cell) first, cores last; two plots meeting → the lower.
* Everything else only banks to those at ≤ 1 : 3 (towards the original ground) — no mounds, no other change.
* `Sculpt.Resnap` puts every object/surface/net of the mall on the final ground.
* Lot art: commercial lots default to *medians* planting (trees in islands, tiers on slopes); strip-mall lots get two
  **walkways** (footpath entrances) from the lot to the shop fronts across the lane (classic, rear alley).

## Tests

`Tests/Placemaker.Tests/LotGradingTests.cs` (54): functional pieces never optional; the retry never excludes stall
markings; markings == stall count (parking, parking/green, strip mall, shopping plaza court); lot dressing ≤ 1/5 of the
stalls; flat ground never graded (every commercial type + parking); parking on cross / steep / gentle ground (on the real
raster): every terraform sloped, ≤ 2 % of bays fall > 2 % front-to-back (none > 5 %), ≤ 1/4 tilt along their row (side
rows on the ramp, all ≤ 8.5 %), drive aisles ≤ 15 % locally, steps ≤ 1.5 m, bays on the ground; every commercial type ×
preset on cross and steep ground: plots at their road's grade (±0.3 m on nine tenths of each plot, all ±0.8 m), no fence
within 1 m of a plot, lanes ≤ 13 % along and ≤ 10 % across away from street junctions, no mounds the original ground did
not have, stalls == markings. `DecalsTests.ParkingOilAlignsWithTheBayAndTyreMarksWithTheAisle` updated (oil ≤ 15 % of bays).

Full suite: 2803 passed.

## Gallery

New `--slopes gentle,cross,steep,hilly` (`Tests/Placemaker.Gallery/Terrain.cs` `SlopeSites`): every land job once per
sloped site, final ground = the real raster, hillshade, plus the s20 `-3d.png` oblique view with three sections; the
summary line prints the grading facts.

Looked at: `--showcase --only stripMall,shoppingPlaza,outdoorMall,shopsPark,parking --no-features` flat and
`--slopes gentle,cross,steep` (all presets): parking terraces with ramped perimeter road and banks only at the outline;
strip mall lot in two tiers, lane + plots level at the lane, a 1 : 3 bank behind the plots into the pocket park; outdoor
mall plots level with the street; decals: ~7 % oil, a handful of stains/tyres per lot.

Known (visible in renders): on 8 % ground the lot rows' 2 % cross-fall leaves up to ~2 m of cut/fill at the row ends; a
lane leg beside a lot near its street junction can still fall up to ~8–10 % across where the lot's front tier is not at the
street's level (the lot is graded before the lanes); 24 m plots on 6–8 % ground cut 1.5–2 m at the back (that is what a
building would dig anyway — now as a planted bank, not a walled pit).

## In-game checks

1. Build a Parking lot (flat) via the ghost/instant path: every stall shows its lines; log `parking: N of N stall markings
   built`. Same with a normal build (terraform present).
2. A lot on a hillside: rows are level terraces, green banks between them, the perimeter road ramps; no lumps under stalls.
   Cars still park (stall lanes connected) on every terrace.
3. The lot reads as dark asphalt (G87 VA surface); if the asset looks wrong, report — fallback is EAI Concrete Asphalt.
4. Decals: a few faint oil blots, no field of dark blobs.
5. Strip mall on a slope: no white cones at the lane/lot junctions; shops grow on the plots without construction pits
   or retaining walls; the bank behind the shops is grass.
6. Log: `left out N rejected parts` must not list the stall markings (`kept despite collision errors` if any).
