# s41 — strip mall entrances: the lane leaves the street flush, no bowl

Playtest (screenshots 71, 72): where a strip mall's lane joins the existing street ("Pine Lane" off Chester Lane,
"Rosewood Lane" off Caraway Street) the ground dipped into a deep bowl:
* the lot asphalt slumped into a pit right at the entrance;
* the grass banks folded;
* the lane sat up on a torn vertical terrain curtain beside the street;
* fence pieces hung over the gap.

Stall rows away from the entrance were fine. The log read `terraform reached its target … 0/2606 texels off, worst
0.17 m`, so the bowl was in the planned target, not a build failure.

## Cause

Reproduced on flat ground with the streets on a 1.2 m fill or in a 1 m cut, and on the s25 cross slope (the gallery's
new `--slopes fill,cut`).

1. **The lane's street end was pinned to the raw terrain under the street.** `MallGrading.Profile.Of` set an attached
   end to `ctx.H(point)`, but a street on a fill or in a cut sits at its own level. The lane started a metre below or
   above the street node. CS2 builds the node at the street's height anyway, which leaves the curtain.
2. **The strip between the site outline and the kerb was never graded.** The sculpt was free and clipped to the
   outline only. Between a street at its level and a lane or lot tied to it (s29) lay a 2–6 m strip of raw ground.
   The build's 4 m bank blended into it: a trough exactly at the entrance, which became the bowl where the lane, the
   lot edge and the street corner meet.
3. **The lot tied its edges to the lane at raw-terrain heights.** The parking sub-plan sees the planned lanes as
   synthetic roads; their heights were the raw ground (`Ctx.P3`), not the lane's graded level.

## Fix (`Core/Layers/Commercial/MallGrading.cs`, `Mall.cs`)

* `MallGrading.StreetHeight` reads the existing street's own height at the attach parameter (the captured curve).
  * Profile samples on the street and its kerb (up to street half width + 1 m) are pinned to that height.
  * From there the lane ramps at ≤ 8 % (the existing grade limit), so the lane leaves the street flush and makes one
    smooth ramp to the site.
* `MallGrading.Approaches`: for every attached lane end, a quad from 2.5 m inside the street's edge to 14 m past it.
  It is the lane's width plus shoulders.
  * The sculpt is free there and the emit clips include it, so the strip between outline and kerb is graded with the
    lane.
  * Everything beside the lane banks to it at ≤ 1:3.
* `Mall.Lot` gives the parking sub-plan the lanes at their preliminary graded heights (the same street-pinned,
  grade-limited profile, without lots). The lot's s29 edge ties now meet the lane's real level.
* Fences: no strip mall plan puts a fence on or across a lane. This is now tested. The fences in the screenshots run
  along the existing streets, so they belong to them.

## Strip mall fingerprints

Re-recorded: 12 entries changed, the sloped `cross`/`steep` site of every preset. The 24 flat-site entries are unchanged
(streets at grade: nothing to correct).

The change is heights only. A planar fingerprint was compared between master and this branch and was identical for all
36 cases. It covers:
* object xz, yaw and asset;
* net end xz and attachments;
* surface and zoning xz;
* facts except grade*/terrain*;
* issues and zone cells.

Only terraform rings and the y of objects, nets and surfaces moved.

## Tests

`Tests/Placemaker.Tests/StripMallEntranceTests.cs` covers every strip mall preset × {streets on fill, streets in cut,
6 % cross slope}:
* every lane end on a street is flush at the kerb (±0.3 m of the street's own height);
* the stub then ramps ≤ 8 % (+ raster tolerance) over its first 15 m;
* no hollow anywhere on the site or in the approaches (3 m neighbourhood, 10 cm);
* no fence net or fence piece within the lane's width + 0.5 m.

`LotGradingTests` commercial plot bound ±0.80 → ±0.85 m: one outdoor-mall corner plot beside a pedestrian street, now
pinned to the street level at its junction, reached 0.80 m. Full suite: 3449 passed. Mod build: 0 errors.

## Gallery

New `--slopes fill,cut`: flat ground, streets on a 1.2 m fill or in a 1 m cut. I looked at
`--showcase --only stripMall --no-features --slopes fill,cut,cross,steep` (3D and sections). The lanes leave the street
flush and ramp into the site, the lot edge meets the street level, and there is no hollow at any entrance.
