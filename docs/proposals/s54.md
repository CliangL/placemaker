# s54 - zoning refresh after a build with its own roads

Bug: market square (own lanes + existing streets); after Build the lawn notches had no zone grid: refresh matched 10 of 70
planned cells, the blocker then touched 3 of those.

Cause (from the log; the 10 matched cells were on existing, unmoved street grids): the cells of the build's OWN lanes were
planned on PREDICTED blocks (ZoneBlockPredictor models free ends only; the game trims/aligns blocks at the lane's junctions),
so the live blocks sit anywhere along the road (up to half a cell off). Refresh took cells by centre inside the plot quad
(cells shrunk 1 m), which on a shifted grid loses columns or whole plots; the unmatched plots were kept as "unsnapped" holes
(no cells there, so nothing zoned). Blocker rings also could touch kept cells after ring repair (CutHoles/PrepareSurface).

Fix (Core/Build/ZoneGuard.cs only; BuildService.ZoneBlock.cs just logs LastRefreshReport):
- Refresh counts planned cells per plot, then grows each plot quad (0.25 m steps, <= 4 m) until the live cells it takes
  equal the planned count; planned == refreshed whenever the live blocks hold the cells.
- A plot no live cell reaches (blocks exist around it) is dropped: its land goes to the blockers, the preview shows it blocked.
- Compute step 3 verifies blockers with the game's test (cell shrunk 0.03 m vs ring); on a touch the keep margin grows
  (+0.3 m, 5 tries), last resort drops the touching ring. ZoneGuard.TouchesAny is public for tests.
Tests: ZoneGuardLaneTests (shifted live block theory, can't-zone plot, scattered plots blocker test).
