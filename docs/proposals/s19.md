# s19 — Zoned buildings grew over a plaza

Playtest (screenshot): a plaza in a block between three streets; big brick apartment/commercial buildings grew right
over it — the buildings stand on the paving and the plaza wraps around them. User: "The zoning doesn't seem to really
work. It creates the cutouts, but maybe it's not doing it right. I think the roads are just overpowering it — it's like
they don't even see the park. There's another mod that adds visible sections — Platter. That should be how it's done."

## 1. Diagnosis (decompiled 1.6.2f1 — RESEARCH-NOTES-ADDENDUM 67–72)

* Every road owns zone blocks: 8 m cells, up to 6 deep (48 m) on each side. A cell stops being zonable only when
  `Game.Zones.CellBlockJobs` marks it **Blocked**: under ground nets with `CompositionState.BlockZone`, or under an area
  whose **prefab** is `PhysicalGeometry` (any lot prefab without `m_AllowOverlap`).
* Nothing a park/plaza build put there was physical: the owner lot "Placemaker Area" allows overlap on purpose (it must
  not collide with our own parts), surfaces (paving, lawn) and pedestrian/hangaround spaces never block. Only our
  pathway nets blocked the cells right under them. So the roads' blocks stayed **Visible and zoned under the whole
  plaza** — with the zoning the player had painted before (our zoning never unzones: Marquee without Overwrite), or any
  zoning painted later. Spawnable buildings only need visible zoned cells → they grew over the paving; surfaces never
  collide with buildings, so the plaza "wrapped round them". The apartment buildings were residential/mixed, i.e. not
  even our low-commercial cutout zone.
* The cutouts themselves were planned as quads along the outline, not on the road's cell grid: the game zones a cell
  when its *centre* is in the quad, so a cutout could gain or lose a column; and when the outline was drawn a few metres
  off the kerb, row 0 (between the road and the outline) was never zoned, so that column could never grow a building.
* Depth: the edgeZoning planner already limited cutout quads to the cutout depth (`FitDepth` on the cutout region), but
  nothing stopped the cells *behind* a cutout (rows deeper than the cutout, under the park) from being zoned by the
  player and used.
* Timing was not the cause (zoning runs 24 frames after Apply; it only needs the road blocks).

## 2. Platter

* Source: **github.com/lucarager/CS2-Platter** (Luca Rager), `Platter/LICENSE.txt` = **MIT** (GPL-3.0 compatible with
  attribution). Installed copy: `%USERPROFILE%\AppData\LocalLow\Colossal Order\Cities Skylines II\.cache\Mods\pdx_mods\125278_49`.
* How it works: a parcel is a static object prefab (lot width × depth) that owns its own `Game.Zones.Block`
  (`BuildOrder 0` so it wins overlaps with road blocks, `ValidArea` = the lot, cells outside the lot Blocked). Because the
  vanilla cell jobs assume road blocks, it runs its own copy of the vanilla CellCheck pipeline after `CellCheckSystem`
  (normalise parcel cells, CleanBlockedCells, an 845-line overlap job), re-registers vanilla SubBlock serialization, links
  each parcel to the nearest road edge (buildings need road access; ≤ 8.4 m in front), toggles spawning, and patches the
  bulldozer and object tool with Harmony. ~11 k lines.
* Verdict: **(c) + no dependency.** Depending on Platter (optional) would not fix the reported problem — road blocks under
  a park would still be zonable — and building our own parcels means replicating vanilla cell jobs exactly, custom
  serialization and Harmony: fragile across game updates, and save compatibility on removal is hard. Every current type's
  cutouts face a road whose blocks exist (streets) or that the build adds (Commercial lanes, which get vanilla blocks), so
  parcels are not needed. Revisit only for plots that face no road at all.

## 3. Fix

### Core — `Core/Build/ZoneGuard.cs` (runs at the end of `Composer.Compose`, after SiteSafety)

* `ComposedPlan.Footprints`: the region of the top-most **non-loose** fill/split step (a park, a plaza, a parking lot, a
  commercial type, each park block of a Roads + Parks recipe). Road grids (loose) have none → never blocked.
* `SiteContext.ZoneGrids`: the zone blocks of existing roads near the area (captured by `SiteCapture`).
* Per block column: the zoned rows are 0 … the deepest cell whose centre lies in a zoning quad (the Marquee rule), at
  least **2 rows** (vanilla blocks row 0 when row 1 is blocked); cells in front of the cutout may be zoned only when they
  lie outside the footprint (the strip between outline and kerb); a column with park in front, or a building lot in the
  run, is not zoned. Snapped quads are **replaced by exact cell rectangles** (shrunk 1 m) in the quad's own layer.
  Quads with no captured cells (a lane this build adds) are kept as planned.
* `ComposedPlan.ZoneBlockers` = footprint inset 0.5 m − zoned cell squares (+0.5 m) − unsnapped quads (+0.5 m) −
  existing building lots (+0.5 m), holes opened (`Poly.CutHoles`), rings made triangulation-safe (`AreaRings.PrepareSurface`).
* Park content on zoned cells that reach past a cutout (front strip, a cell centred inside the cutout) is cleared like the
  cutout itself (`Cutouts.Enforce(plan, rings)`).
* `ComposedPlan.ZoneCells`: every captured cell the build zones / blocks (incl. CleanBlockedCells propagation) / keeps
  (existing building) — for the preview and tests.
* Option **"Keep zoned buildings off builds"** (Options → Build, default on) = `ZoneGuard.Enabled` / `BuildService.BlockZoning`.

### Game

* `OwnerAreaPrefabSystem` registers a second runtime lot prefab **"Placemaker Zone Block"**: `m_AllowOverlap = false`
  (→ PhysicalGeometry → blocks cells), fully transparent, no CanOverrideObjects. Registered at startup like the owner
  lot so saves resolve it by name.
* `BuildService.ZoneBlock` — new step **after the main Apply settled, before the zoning**: emit one definition per
  blocker ring, wait for the temporaries, validate; a piece under a building the game would demolish (Warning) is left
  out and the rest re-emitted (≤ 3 rounds; else no blocking); tag `PlacemakerMember{Auxiliary, ZoneBlocker}`; Apply.
  Its overlap errors (our paths, fountains, the owner lot) are ignored — Apply builds errored temporaries — and they never
  reach the main build's exclusion logic. The zoning step then waits 24 frames, so the Marquee only finds cells the
  blockers left visible.
* `SiteCapture` captures `Game.Zones.Block`s (search tree, ≤ 4000) into `SiteContext.ZoneGrids`.
* Removal: blockers are members (Auxiliary) → removed with the group → the game unblocks the cells. Grown shops stay
  (they are city buildings). Zoning the player painted *before* the build comes back with the cells (as when a vanilla
  road is removed).
* Overlay (`PlacemakerOverlaySystem.DrawZoneCells`): zoned cells filled in the zone's colour (commercial blue,
  residential green, office purple, industrial yellow) with a solid outline; blocked cells a faint red fill, dashed
  outline and a diagonal; cells of existing buildings a white outline. Shown also while the ghost hides the sketch. Without
  captured blocks the blocker outline is drawn dashed red.

### Tradeoffs

* The blocker is physical: after the build, roads/props/paths the player places across it report "overlaps existing"
  (like a vanilla building lot), and a road upgrade that widens into the park is refused. Edit/rebuild removes the old
  group first, so Placemaker's own rebuilds are unaffected. The option turns it off.
* 1-row cutouts become 2 rows deep (8 m into the park, cleared of park content) — otherwise they could never grow.

## 4. Tests

`Tests/Placemaker.Tests/ZoneGuardTests.cs` (17): cell geometry = ZoneUtils; a cutout gets exactly its cells (3 × 2)
and everything else of the park is blocked (front cells and the row behind the cutout); misaligned quad snaps by centre;
1-row cutout → 2 rows; front strip between outline and kerb zoned; quad behind park land not zoned and kept free;
existing building lot keeps its cells (and the blocker stays off it); a building inside a cutout is never zoned over;
no captured blocks → quads kept and kept free; no cutouts → all blocked; far blocks ignored; `Apply` replaces snapped
quads and clears park content on zoned cells; no footprint → nothing; plaza / park / market square composed between four
streets with captured blocks: blockers never touch a zoned cell, no zoning quad takes a blocked cell, rings acceptable.

## 5. In-game verification

1. Zone a whole block (any zone), then build a **plaza** with Zoning (Row, some) on it. Preview: blue cells along the
   streets = cutouts, faint red hatched cells = the rest of the block under the plaza. After the build, open the zoning
   tool: the plaza shows **no zone cells** except the cutouts; old zoning outside the plaza untouched.
2. Let the city run: shops grow only in the cutouts, facing their street, no deeper than the cutout; nothing grows on the
   paving. Try painting zoning over the plaza: the cells are not paintable.
3. Log: "Placemaker lot 'Placemaker Zone Block' is ready (flags PhysicalGeometry, …)" at startup and
   "N zone block lot(s) keep zoned buildings off group G" per build. No "zone blocking would remove existing buildings".
4. Build over a block with an existing house inside the outline: the house stays (no condemned icon), its cells stay
   zoned; the cells behind it under the park are blocked.
5. Cutout with the outline drawn ~6 m off the kerb: the cutout still grows buildings (row 0 is zoned).
6. Bulldoze the plaza (one click on the owner): the blockers go with it; cells become zonable again (with the zone they
   had before); shops that grew in the cutouts stay.
7. Save, reload (with the mod): blocking persists. Load without the mod: the unknown lot prefab keeps blocking or is
   dropped by the game (either is acceptable; report which).
8. Selecting the plaza still selects the owner lot (white); the blockers are never visible, not even when selected.
9. Commercial strip mall / shopping plaza (lanes): plots along the new lanes zone and grow; the lot and lanes do not.
10. Roads + Parks: the park blocks are blocked except their cutouts; the plain road grid zoning (edge zoning) stays.
11. Option off ("Keep zoned buildings off builds"): no blockers, s14 behaviour.
12. After a build, try a path/prop/road across the park: expect "overlaps existing" (by design; document if annoying).
