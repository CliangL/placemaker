# s27 — brushes keep off other Placemaker builds; one set of brush rules for every paint tool

Playtest (screenshot): a new area painted next to an already-built Placemaker park. The outline stopped at the
roads but ran into the existing park, over its paths, playground and trees. User: "I don't want that by default —
but I might, so settings. Settings for paint to paint on anything. Settings for all the paint tools."

## Why builds were not obstacles

- **Plain Paint** (the screenshot: its "Drag to paint / Right-drag to erase" hints) knew only roads (keep-off-roads
  clip) and, since s15, buildings. It never looked at lots or areas at all.
- **Draw / Roads / Block** never cut outlines against anything but roads.
- **Block paint** did read lot areas from the area search tree, including our owner lot "Placemaker Area" (a
  `LotPrefab` → `Game.Areas.Lot`) and the s19 "Placemaker Zone Block" lots. It counted them as generic "lots"
  under the "Areas" rule, so it already stopped there unless that rule was off. There was also no fallback when the
  owner lot is not found.

## What changed

- **New obstacle kind `PlacemakerBuild`** (`Game/Selection/BlockObstacles`):
  - Lot areas and buildings whose entity belongs to a Placemaker group (`IGroupRegistry.GroupOf`): owner lot,
    zone-block lots, the hidden companion building.
  - The outline of every group's receipt (decoded once per group, cached) as a fallback.
  - The group being edited never counts.
- **Shared brush rules** (`Core/Geometry/BrushRules`), all persisted (Options → "Brush rules (Paint, Block paint, Draw)")
  and in the panel:
  - New stop **Other builds** (`Builds`, setting `BrushStopBuilds`, default **on**).
  - New master **Paint over anything** (`Anything`, setting `BrushPaintOverAnything`, default off). It turns every
    stop, water and the block lock off. Block paint then becomes plain unfenced painting (`BlockBrush.Free`), and
    Paint becomes the old Paint (no road clip, no cut).
  - New **Draw: clip to free land** (`DrawClip`, setting `BrushDrawClip`, default off).
  - Existing ids kept: `BrushStopRoads/Buildings/Lots/Zoned`, `BlockPaintAvoidEmptyZones`, `BrushStopWater`,
    `BrushStayInBlock`, `BrushSnapDistance`, `BrushSmoothing`, `ResetBrushRules`.
  - `PaintAvoidBuildings` (s15) is hidden and no longer read: the "Buildings" rule replaces it. A player who had
    it off now gets buildings cut again unless they turn the rule off.
  - The brush options were never localised on the Options page (missing from `ToolRegistration`'s list). Fixed.
- **Plain Paint honours the rules:**
  - Roads: its keep-off-roads clip now follows the "Roads" stop instead of the Draw option.
  - Buildings, other builds, lots, zoned and empty zone cells: cut out of the outline on mouse-up (`BrushRules.Cut`
    → `ObstacleCut`, enclosed obstacles opened with a 1 m corridor).
  - Water: cells under water are dropped before tracing the outline.
  - The block lock, snap and smoothing are Block paint only. They are greyed in Paint with the reason.
  - Because the rules are shared, Paint now also keeps off zoned cells, lots and water by default, not only
    roads, buildings and other builds.
- **Draw / Roads (perimeter) / Block** (`PlacemakerToolSystem.KeepOff`):
  - The outline is always cut back from other Placemaker builds (0.5 m margin), unless "Other builds" or "Paint
    over anything" says otherwise.
  - With "clip to free land" it is also cut back from every active polygon stop (not water).
  - A cut block or road-loop outline becomes `Painted`, because it is no longer all road frontage.
  - An outline lying entirely on other builds gives no area, with the message "This outline lies on another
    Placemaker build…".
  - Obstacles are gathered once per region (and every 5 s), not on each drag rebuild.
- **Overlay:**
  - Block paint shades other builds like every stop ("won't paint here").
  - Paint shades every active stop near the brush while painting (it used to shade buildings only).
  - Draw / Roads / Block shade the builds the outline was cut against, and Draw shows the cut outline.
- **UI:**
  - Rules row: "Over all" master toggle (new `paintAny` icon) and an "Other builds" stop (new `otherBuilds` icon).
    It is shown in both Paint and Block paint; stops are greyed while "Paint over anything" is on.
  - Draw / Block / Roads: a "Rules" section with a "Keep off" row: "Other builds" and "Free land" toggles.
- **Contract (additive, PROTOCOL 19 → 20):** `BrushRules.builds?`, `anything?`, `drawClip?`. Same
  `setBrushRules` JSON.

## Tests

- `GeometryBlockBrushTests`:
  - Other builds stop the Block paint brush, and the edge snaps onto the build's outline.
  - Other builds are independent of the Areas rule.
  - "Paint over anything" turns every stop, water and the lock off, and the brush is unconstrained.
  - `Cut` for plain Paint: builds and zoned cells cut; with builds off, only zoned.
  - `Cut` for Draw's default (builds only), and an outline entirely on a build → null with a reason.
  - JSON round trip of the new fields.
- The existing "all off = Paint" test now also turns "Other builds" off.
- Mock: the Paint rules row (lock greyed, builds on), the "Paint over anything" greying, and Draw's "Keep off" row.

## Needs an in-game check

1. The screenshot case: paint next to a built park → the outline stops at the park's lot edge (Block paint snaps
   onto it); turn on "Paint over anything" → it paints over the park as before.
2. Editing a group (Edit → Area) and repainting/redrawing it: its own lot must not block.
3. Draw a polygon overlapping a built plaza: the outline is cut back, the plaza is shaded, the build does not
   touch the plaza. An outline drawn completely on the plaza → the "lies on another build" problem.
4. Zone-block lots (s19) next to a cutout: the cutouts (zoned cells, now under "Zoned") are kept off too.
5. Other mods' lots ("Areas") and vanilla park buildings ("Buildings").
6. Options page: the group title and all labels show text (the brush options had raw ids before).
