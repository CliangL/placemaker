# s32 — Commercial: outdoor malls, mall districts, parks inside malls, big boxes, market streets

User: "Even more strip-mall-type stuff: outdoor malls, advanced malls, crazy stuff, parks inside of malls, one that's just
all a mall, big buildings, little buildings. Strip malls are perfect — don't refine that. Just do another pass, make it
amazing."

The **Strip mall is untouched**: same file, same presets, and `StripMallRegressionTests` fingerprints every preset on four
flat test sites and two sloped ones (every object, net, surface, terraform, zoning quad, region, fact, issue, zone cell
and zone blocker) against hashes recorded on master before any s32 change (`Tests/Placemaker.Tests/Fixtures/
stripmall-fingerprints.txt`). Every shared-kit change below is either new code the strip mall never calls or gated on a
per-lane / per-mall setting the strip mall leaves at its old default.

**Street shop zone** `outerZone` (default high commercial in the district, mall park, grand and lifestyle presets),

## The kit (Core/Layers/Commercial/MallKit.cs, partial class Mall)

* **Lane networks.** `Connect(End a, inner, End b)` — an end is an existing street (outline point + outward), a point on
  another of the mall's lanes (T or X junction: the host is split there at Finish, identical node) or free (a dead end,
  only for pedestrian streets ending on their plaza). `Loop()` = a closed ring. `Remove()` takes a lane back. Corridors
  of lane-on-lane ends stop at the host's edge.
* **Rows.** `Row()` scans the frontage for free stretches (1 m probes, bounding-box prefiltered) and spreads pieces evenly
  over each; pieces are at least `maxPiece` cells long and two cells deep. `LaneRows()` runs rows along every segment of
  a lane, trims bends, keeps `JunctionClear` (facing tolerance + 0.5 m) from crossing lanes and starts rows on the
  lane's zone-cell offset (`ZoneUtils.GetCellWidth · 4`: 8 m for the 16 m Small Road). `StreetRows()` lays rows facing
  existing streets a whole cell off every lane. `ShopZone` gives a plot its own zone (kind or prefab).
* **Walks, paseos, gateways.** `Walk()` lays a path between lanes (joined at nodes) and takes its strip; `DressPaseo()`
  paves it with a lamp and planters; `Hold()/Release()` keep gateways and squares free while rows are laid; `Green()`
  plants verges and gateway strips with trees that keep clear of every net and object; `FillOpen()` runs a park/plaza
  sub-plan whose walks are stretched onto lane nodes (and can carve its feature places for `AddOn()` to fill);
  `AddOn()` runs a feature (playground, fountain, kiosks, food stands) on a piece as a sub-plan that sees everything
  planned so far; `GreenRest()` turns what is left into parks opening onto the lanes and planted strips.
* **Lots.** Unchanged `Mall.Lot`, plus: a stub or footpath hitting a lane past a bend is snapped exactly onto the lane
  line (ends already on it are left bit-for-bit alone); joins along a 16 m road keep ≥ 20 m apart (a piece shorter than
  two cells gets no zone block); stubs keep 8 m from paseo footpaths.

### Zoning rules the compositions follow (s26, decompiled BlockSystem/ZoneGuard)

1. A plot is zoned by the road whose block reaches it at the shallowest row; ties go to our lanes → a plot meant to face a
   street keeps a whole cell (`Mall.Gap` = 8 m) from every lane; lane mouths get planted gateway strips.
2. CS2 blocks row 0 when row 1 is blocked → plots are two cells deep: a one-cell row is zoned two rows deep, into the plot
   behind it (found by the tests: "plot has no zoning").
3. Any 8 m of frontage holds one cell centre of a block, whatever its alignment → pieces are at least a cell long.
4. A corner plot touching two crossing lanes is zoned by two crossing grids → rows keep `JunctionClear` from crossing lanes.
5. A net touching a zone cell closes it and every cell behind → a park walk never dead-ends at a plot's back
   (`KeepWalksOffPlots`: pulled back 3.5 m, or the stub removed), suggested park entrances only where people arrive
   (`Arrival`: beside a street, lane or walk, never on a plot edge).
6. Pedestrian streets / alleys only when the catalog does not say they carry no zone blocks (`PedLane`, `ShopLane`,
   `ServiceRoad` fall back to a road that does).
7. Per-plot zone kinds resolve to prefabs in `Finish` (a kind id there zones nothing in game — caught by a test).

## Grading (MallGrading) — slopes

* Lanes ending on another lane are pinned to the host's profile (hosts graded first); a closed loop meets itself.
* Per-lane `Shoulder` (s32 lanes 4 m, levelled beside the lane) and `MaxGrade` (s32 lanes 6 %); where shouldered lanes
  meet, the **nearest** lane sets a node (no level platform from a crossing lane across a sloping one); a shouldered lane
  also levels one cell of a plot that faces another lane. Strip mall lanes keep the old values (default −1 / 0).
* `Mall.GradeTolerance` (s32 types 0.04 m instead of 0.12): lanes running up a slope otherwise kept 0.1 m lumps of the
  original ground in every sculpt cell the profile happened to come close to (13–17 % local climbs in the raster).
* **Slope banks** (`Mall.SlopeGap`: one cell where the fitted site plane falls > 2 %): street rows keep a planted bank from
  lane rows, district blocks get at least a cell of courtyard between back-to-back rows, mall-park access lanes keep a
  bank from the ring plots, lifestyle lots keep 4 m from the ring. On level sites nothing changes (and nothing is graded).
* Speed: plot and lane node loops visit only their bounding boxes (same order, identical result; a 5 ha district went
  from ~760 ms to ~40 ms of grading in Debug); ZoneGuard prefilters its cell-centre-in-quad tests by quad bounds.

## Tests

* `StripMallRegressionTests` — strip mall unchanged (36 fingerprints, master baseline).
* `CommercialTests` / `LotGradingTests` — now over all eight commercial types (sites below a type's minimum area are
  skipped): every plot zoned and facing a street or lane, no overlapping plots, lanes attached to real streets, bays and
  drive aisles off plots, nothing on a plot, surfaces accepted; on cross (6 %) and steep (8 % diagonal) ground plots at
  their road's grade, lanes ≤ 13 % along / ≤ 10 % across, no mounds, no fences near plots, stalls = markings. The
  grading test now measures a lane's facing tolerance with its real width (a pedestrian street is 8 m, not the 16 m
  street default).
* `CommercialS32Tests` — every s32 type × preset × six sites (four test sites + 2.5 and 4 ha squares with the gallery's
  catalog and layout scale): **no plot dropped as unzonable**, lanes carry zone blocks, every lane network reaches a street,
  car lanes never dead-end, walks meet lanes at nodes, stalls = markings; big plots on the streets and small ones on the
  lanes with their own zones; lot → paseo → pedestrian street walking routes; the mall park has its playground and walks;
  determinism; 2–5 presets; level sites never graded.
* Full suite: see the commit / report (timing tests may flake under load; they pass alone).

## In-game checks

1. **Zone blocks**: log at load `zone blocks by road: Alley …, Pedestrian Street Small …` — if a pedestrian street says NO,
   the high street / district / lifestyle lanes fall back to the shop lane automatically; confirm the plots then zone.
2. **High street** on a 2–4 ha block: the market strip between two pedestrian streets; shops grow facing the market;
   walkers from the side lots come through the paseos (`zoning: plot i … zones from 'Pedestrian Street Small'`).
3. **Mall district**: outer plots grow high-density buildings facing the streets (not the rails — the gateway strip keeps
   them a cell off); inner lanes grow small low-density shops; the X junction has its four squares; courtyards reachable.
4. **Mall park**: the loop is one closed lane (no dead-end caps), park walks join it at the access junctions; the park's
   fountain plaza and playground present; inner shops face the park, outer ones the streets.
5. **Retail park**: the 16 m service road zones the boxes (each road piece between stubs ≥ 20 m — log per plot); the lot
   has entrances from the street and the road; walkway to the back terrace; pads on the main street.
6. **Lifestyle centre**: ring drive U, lots outside it with stalls marked, the paseo footpaths of the lots, the ring and the
   rows meet at single nodes (no 10 cm gaps); plaza fountain; park and playground behind.
7. **Slopes**: plots at their road's height with grass banks between rows (no construction pits); lanes ≤ 6 % graded,
   smooth through junctions.
8. Per-plot zones: the zoning view shows high-density commercial on the street plots and low-density on the lanes.
