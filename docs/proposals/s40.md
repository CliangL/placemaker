# s40 — zoning first: shop plots are the cells

Playtest (image 70): a built market square with paving notches, grass leftovers and gaps around the shops that grew;
in Design the plots (yellow/green outlines) and the blue zone cells did not coincide — cells covering part of a plot,
poking out of others, plots without cells. User: "Shouldn't it be snug against where it knows it can zone? Can we
coagulate these systems?"

## Design

* `Core/Build/ZoneLots.cs` (new). `SiteGrids(site, planned)` = the blocks the zoning will meet: captured blocks, the
  predicted blocks of planned lanes, and — only when nothing was captured (tests, gallery) — predicted blocks of the
  existing streets. `Snap(footprints, plots, grids, nets, own, taken)` runs ZoneGuard's own computation on all candidate
  plots together (dedupe, widening, the game's depth rule, ≥ 2 columns) and returns, per candidate, the exact cell
  rectangles (curb-flush, whole cells, rows from row 0). `CellsTag` marks a plot that IS cells.
* **Zoning feature** (parks, plazas, market squares…; `StreetShops.Plan`): the layouts still choose where shops go
  (amount, pattern, depth, entrance gaps); then every plot becomes its cells; a plot with none goes back to the host.
  The carve = the cells grown 2.5 m (`ZoneLots.Clearance`; paths and paving keep clear: a path edge touching a cell closes its column, and 2.5 m keeps the re-flowed park layouts' pads flat on rough sites), pushed to 1 m past
  the kerb on the road side (a 12 m street counts 16 m in zoning; the strip between kerb and row 0 is not host land).
  The host plans around exactly that. Regions are tagged `zoneCells`; `EdgeZoningAddOn` zones such a region as it is
  (its rectangle shrunk 1 m — the Marquee takes precisely those cells) instead of re-deriving quads from its edges.
* **Commercial kit**: `Mall.SnapStreetPlotsToCells` after the type composed its layout and before the rest is planned
  (`CommercialLayer.PlanCore`): street-facing plots become their cells (land already given to lots, lanes, walks and
  places is protected; lane plots are zoned in the same computation, so the depth rule sees their cells), the rest's
  parks and verges then fit the cells. Lane-facing plots keep their rows: a lane's cells depend on where the rest's
  walks will join (and split) it — ZoneGuard snaps them at the end (known limit: lane plots are not yet cells at plan time). `ShopsPark` takes StreetShops' cell plots as they are.
* Speckled pads are ≥ 2 cells wide (a 1-cell pad is cut to nothing by the depth rule). `Cutouts` border clearing
  reaches the carve clearance (`BorderReach`), the outline exemption stays at 1 m (`OutlineReach`).
* **ZoneGuard**: a quad that is exactly one block's cells is zoned by that block only (`AlignedGrid`), and blocks a cell
  plot was snapped to rank first — at a street corner the crossing street's block never takes the plot's land. Cell
  dedupe tightened (overlap beyond 0.1 m). The later re-snap therefore finds the cells as planned (no drift; the
  in-game refresh logs any).
* Side fix: playground benches skip ground steeper than the furniture limit (a layout shift put two on a bank).

Strip mall: fingerprints re-recorded. **corner** (all sites) and the **cross / steep** sites of default, classic,
rearAlley, neighbourhood and bigBox changed: those are the sites whose layouts have street-facing plots off the main
frontage grid (crossing street / slope-shifted rows); the plots are now the street's cells and the planted rest and
clearance follow. Lane-facing layouts and all other sites are unchanged.

## Tests

`ZoningFirstTests` (53): for plaza / market square / park with the Zoning feature on straight and angled (23°) sites,
a plaza on a curved four-lane street, and every commercial type and preset (street plots; lane plots exempt): plot
region == union of its zoned cells (area equal, no cell corner outside), no zoned cell outside the plots, no host object
or net on a zoned cell; no "not zonable" issue for the feature hosts. Streets' blocks are "captured" in these sites
(as in the game). Updated: `LeisureTests.StreetShopsZoneTheFrontageAndTheParkKeepsOut` (row 0 of a 12 m street starts
2 m past the kerb). Gallery `--showcase` renders of plaza / market square / park / shopping plaza / high street.

## In-game checks

1. Market square / plaza with shops: Design shows the plot outlines exactly on the blue cells; paving and lawns end at
   the cells (2.5 m clearance); after the build the buildings fill the plots without paving notches or grass leftovers.
2. Log after the build: `zoning refresh: … 0 on a moved grid` (no drift) unless park paths split a street edge.
3. A corner plot is zoned by the street it was planned on; no crossing grids.
