# s43 — Paint bucket walls; Road nodes close themselves

Playtest: "Make the Paint bucket more advanced. It's not following paths; some streets it's even ignoring. It goes over
streets and roads and paths. I want to customize what it sees and doesn't see." Lead add-on (screenshot of Road nodes
with three sides picked and an open gap across a field): "Shouldn't it still work even if it's not fully closed —
closable at any moment, it just bridges that gap?" and "cleaner".

## Why the bucket ignored streets and paths (causes)

`RoadNetwork.ComputeBoundary` decided what bounds a block. Found in the code:

1. **Only nets with a `Game.Net.Road` component counted.** Pedestrian paths (`PathwayData` prefabs), bike paths and
   train / tram tracks were invisible, so the fill ran straight over them. (All car roads — alleys, gravel, one-way,
   pedestrian streets — do have `Road`, so they were in.)
2. **Side connections.** In CS2 a path (sometimes a road) can end ON another edge without splitting it
   (`Game.Net.ConnectedNode` on the host edge). In a node-to-node graph such an end dangles, the path is stripped as a
   dead end and the block is not split — even with paths counted, most paths that meet a street at the sidewalk would
   still have been ignored.
3. **Every net owned by any object was dropped** (meant for nets inside building lots). Nets owned by non-building
   objects (asset-pack intersections and the like) disappeared, leaving gaps through which the fill leaked into the
   next block. Now only nets inside a building / extension (owner chain) are dropped.
4. A tree of open-ended paths could in principle be walked as a "face" (area exactly 0 plus rounding > 0); the face
   picker now treats walks below 0.5 m² as trees (`PlanarGraph.TreeAreaEpsilon`).
5. Unchanged by design: bridges and tunnels never bound a block (they cross other nets without a node). A street
   with a bridge in the middle leaves a gap there — turn on Water so the river stops the fill, or use Points.

## Paint bucket walls (`Core/Geometry/FillWalls.cs`, `FillGraph.cs`)

Chips in the Paint bucket settings, "Walls" row (icon + word, same chip as Paint area's stops), above the s42
"Keep off" chips:

| Chip | What | Kind | Default |
|---|---|---|---|
| Roads | every car road (alleys, gravel, one-way, highways, pedestrian streets) | line | on |
| Paths | pedestrian / shared paths | line | **on** (the complaint) |
| Bike paths | bicycle-only nets (bike lane, no car / pedestrian lane) | line | on |
| Rail & tram | train / tram / subway tracks at ground level | line | on |
| Water | water deeper than 0.3 m | area | on |
| Lots | buildings, other lots (parks, extractors, other mods), other Placemaker builds | area | off |
| Zoned | zoned cells | area | off |

* **Line walls** are edges of the planar graph: a path between two roads splits the block exactly like a street.
  Side-connected ends split their host edge (`FillGraph.Split`, sub-edge ids from `SubEdgeId(host, k)`).
  Turning a kind off merges the faces it separated. Open-ended paths inside a block are stripped (no bogus faces).
* **Curb**: blocks of real edges keep the s1 curb outline (entity `EdgeGeometry`, which is each net's own width).
  Blocks with split sub-edges use the centre ring minus each wall's corridor at its own half-width
  (`FillGraph.CurbOutline`; paths and dead ends inside the block are cut out at their width too).
* **Area walls** cut the traced block afterwards and keep **the side you clicked** (`FillWalls.ClipToSeed`); "Keep off"
  keeps the largest piece instead. Water comes from the water predicate as 4 m row-run rectangles
  (`FillWalls.WaterRects`). Lots default off because "Keep off: My builds" (on) already cuts other Placemaker builds,
  and vanilla buildings around a block would otherwise fragment every fill.
* The s42 "Keep off" chips (My builds, Clip to free land) still run after the fill, unchanged.
* Changing walls re-traces the picked blocks from where they were clicked (`BlockSelectionState.Seeds`, new, parallel
  to Faces; snapshots from before s43 use the face centre). It is a setting, not an undo step.
* No line wall enabled → "The bucket needs a line wall…". Click on water/lots only → "Only walls here…".
  Not enclosed → "Not enclosed here. Turn on more walls (paths, tracks…) or use Points."
* Overlay (Area stage, Block mode): the walls the fill traces within 220 m of the cursor, faint and coloured by kind
  (road white, path green, bike teal, track amber); hover face as before (fill + dashed outline).
* Entrances: blocks bounded by tracks get no road attachment on the track side.
* Block paint is unchanged: it still fences to road-only blocks and uses its own stops.

**Persistence**: `PlacemakerSettings.BucketWallRoads/Paths/Bikes/Tracks/Water/Lots/Zoned` (Options → brush group,
localised), mirrored in `BrushRules.BucketWalls`. **Contract (additive, PROTOCOL 21 → 22)**: `BrushRules.walls?:
BucketWalls`; same `setBrushRules` trigger, `{ walls: { paths: false } }` merges per key (`UiModels.ParseBrushRules`).
An older DLL sends no `walls` → the row is hidden.

## Road nodes: closable at any moment, cleaner (`PlacemakerToolSystem.Perimeter.cs`, `RoadGraph.cs`)

* From 3 picked nodes the open chain already is an area (Next / Enter work). The gap from the last node back to the
  first closes **along the roads** when a route exists that avoids the chain's nodes and edges and is at most
  1.5× the straight distance; otherwise with a **straight chord** across open land (`RoadGraphAlgorithms.Closing`,
  `StraightEdge` marker half-edge). The curb outline joins the neighbouring curbs straight across the chord.
* Clicking the first node closes with exactly the previewed closing; right-click on a closed loop drops the whole
  closing again. Keep clicking to extend at any time.
* Overlay: picked roads at ~45 % of the old width, node dots about half size, the closing dashed (along its road or
  straight), the outline thin and dim; the area itself is the faint selection fill. Hint: "The gap closes itself
  (dashed); Next to go on".

## Needs an in-game check

1. A block crossed by a footpath that meets the streets at the sidewalk: the bucket fills one side only; turn Paths
   off → the whole block. Check the log for "block pick failed" / "block outline failed".
2. Bike paths: are they `Road` nets with only bicycle lanes or pathways? Both are handled; check the chip actually
   toggles them (if a bike path still splits with Bike paths off, it is classified as a Path/Road — log `KindOf`).
3. Rail at grade and tram tracks: blocks close along them; no entrance suggested on the track side.
4. Streets previously ignored: if a street still does not bound the fill, note what it is (asset-pack intersection,
   road owned by something) — `OwnedByBuilding` now only drops nets inside buildings.
5. Water wall on a shoreline block: the fill stops at the water and keeps the clicked side; hover cost while moving
   over water (each move re-samples the block box on a 4 m grid).
6. Lots wall: click between houses → the open land between them only; Zoned wall.
7. Corner shape of split-path blocks (corridor outline): sharp corners at curb lines, path cut at its own width.
8. Road nodes: the screenshot case → after the third side the area appears with a dashed chord; Next works; clicking
   the first node closes with the chord; with a short road back the dashed line follows the road.
9. Options page labels for the seven new settings.

## Not done / open

* Bridges / tunnels as walls (they cross without nodes, would break planarity).
* Fences, waterways, power lines are never walls.
* Shift+click unions with area walls keep the piece of the last click only.
