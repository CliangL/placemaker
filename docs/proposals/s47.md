# s47 — mall lane entrances for every MallKit type; InvalidShape on lane pieces and lot connectors

Playtest (screenshot 79): a shopping plaza (U lane off Bayview Street, court preset) — the lot slumped into a bowl where the
right leg meets it; the log read `InvalidShape×8 [net 'Alley' ×6; net 'Invisible Road Path - 2xTwoway' ×2]`.

## 1. The pit: a lane profile that cannot reach its pins

s41 already made every MallKit lane leave its street at the street's height (`MallGrading.Profile.Of`, `Approaches`, `Mall.Lot`
all work on `Mall.Lane`, not on the strip mall alone). The new `MallEntranceTests` (s41's checks × shopping plaza, outdoor mall,
mall park, mall district, retail park, high street, shops & park, every preset × streets on fill / in cut / 6 % cross slope /
8 % diagonal / 2.5 % gentle) found the cause for the rest:

* The profile pins the street end(s) (and a host lane, or a closed loop) and then clamps the grade to the lane's limit
  (6 % for MallKit lanes) in a forward and a backward pass. Where the pins are further apart in height than the limit allows over
  the run between them (a lane down a hillside steeper than 6 %, pinned to the street at one end and to its host at the other)
  the passes cannot keep the limit; the whole difference ended up as one step beside the first free sample: **0.67 m in 2 m,
  7 m from the kerb** (mall park on the cross slope: -0.54 → +0.13 m). That step is the entrance pit.
* Failing on master: high street, mall district and mall park on the cross and steep ground (34 of 200 cases). The shopping plaza and
  the outdoor mall are fine on these sites; the plaza's U is the same code path.

Fix (`MallGrading.Profile.Of`): between two pinned samples a run takes the grade it needs, evenly (`|Δh| / run × 1.02`), the limit
everywhere else. Nothing changes where the pins are feasible: strip mall fingerprints (sloped sites included) are unchanged by it.

## 2. InvalidShape: alley stubs too short for their nodes

Game.Net.ValidationHelpers.ValidateEdge folds an edge whose node geometry is too tight for the widths (RESEARCH-NOTES-ADDENDUM 32).
Each edge end is cut back at a node to where its edge crosses its neighbour's: `(h_other + h_self · cos φ) / sin φ` (a T is
`h_other`; a continuation needs none); the two cut-backs of one edge must fit its length (the model in `ConcentricGenerator`).

The plaza's court lot has two "alley" entrances on the lane legs. The lot's edge lies on the lane's kerb, so the parking planner's
stub (`PlanBuilder`: lane centre → lot edge) is exactly the lane's half width (4 m) — all of it is the T cut-back — and the lot's
connector then turns ~84° at the lot edge towards the nearest aisle end, needing 3.1 m more: 7.1 m. The stub folds (2), the lane's
4 pieces on the two T nodes fold (4) = the 6 "Alley"; the 2 connectors fold at the turn = the 2 "Invisible Road Path - 2xTwoway".

Fix (`Core/Layers/Parking/PlanBuilder.AlleyStub`): the stub is carried on into the lot (≤ 8 m) until both fit; if a stall is in the
way, or the connector would be too short, there is no stub: the connector ends at the kerb as a street entrance does (CS2 connects a
dead end at a kerb: LocalConnect) and the lane is not split (no T node). A straight stub (the strip mall's) is unchanged. In the
plaza's court preset the stalls are in the way, so both stubs became kerb connectors; the court is still entered from the street.
Strip mall "corner" has the same turn: its 6 fingerprints (flat + sloped sites) are re-recorded; no other preset changed.

## Tests

* `MallEntranceTests` (200 cases): lane flush at the kerb (±0.3 m of the street), ramp ≤ 12 % over the stub (the lane may be steeper
  than 8 % where it is pinned at both ends on a hillside), no hollow (3 m neighbourhood, 10 cm), no fence on a lane.
* `MallNetGeometryTests` (46 cases): the cut-back model of the game's edge check on every lane (`Alley`, `Street`) and lot connector of
  all 8 commercial types × presets; fails on master for the plaza court (4.0 m stubs needing 7.1 m) and strip mall "corner".
