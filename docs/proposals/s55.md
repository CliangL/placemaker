# s55 — lot props vanish after a "successful" build; entrance pit diagnosis

Playtest 89/90: preview of a lot shows island trees, bushes, lamps and EV chargers; the built lot has the stall lines only. Log: `parking: 396 of 396 stall
markings built`, `verified group 1: objects 656`, `build audit: planned 818, built 733, left out 85 / declutter x85`, no `anarchy: cleared` line.

## Cause (decompiled 1.6.2f1, Game.Objects.OverrideSystem)
After Apply, OverrideSystem checks every overridable object (trees, bushes, lamps, props, decals — `GeometryFlags.Overridable`): one that meets a non-overridable
thing (net, building, physical area) or loses the pair priority against another overridable object (lower entity index wins) gets `Overridden` = hidden.
Stall markings stopped being sunk in 2b97af4, so every prop whose box touches a 3.2 x 6.2 m marking box is paired with a marking. The pair is skipped only when both
share the same top-level owner (`ObjectIterator.Iterate`: `m_TopLevelEntity == entity`) — which is why markings never hid each other (all `Owner(carrier)`).
The audit counted temporaries (`verified objects`), so it said "built" for objects that were hidden a few frames later. Anarchy never helped: it only clears our
own validation gate, not the post-Apply Overridden. "cleared" is logged only when an Error remains to clear; the second build had none (the first attempt's 399
errors were collisions with the ghost's temporaries of the same lot, 2 s before).

## Fixes
* `BuildService.ShareCarrierOwner` (Adopt.cs): on a build with a parking carrier every plain overridable object (not a building, not owned, not attached) gets
  `Owner(carrier)` at tag time, like the bays. The game no longer pairs it with markings or other props. Log: `N props/trees/chargers share the parking carrier owner`.
* `BuildService.AuditSurvivors` (Audit.cs, in DoFinish ~50 frames after Apply): counts group objects with `Overridden`; the ones hidden only by our own overlap are
  shown again (`Overridden` removed + `BatchesUpdated`), the rest become audit reason `hidden` ("Hidden by the game: overlap"), so "built" no longer counts hidden
  objects. Log: `survivors: N object(s) ... hidden by the game`.
* `ClassifyOwnCollisions`: logs why errors are NOT ignorable (`not-our-temporary`, `no-plan-object`, `no-error-icon`, `type-X`, `overlaps-nothing-planned`,
  `touches-building`).
* `Declutter.Anarchy` (pushed from the setting): on a plan with stall markings only a near-exact duplicate (centres < 0.4 m / a third of the smaller box) is moved or
  dropped; overlaps with markings and neighbouring props are kept (they were 85 dropped bushes/lamps/trees).

## Entrance pit
Not reproducible in the plan: a sweep of every parking/commercial type x rectangular/trapezoid/L sites x streets on 1-2 sides x fill/cut/cross/steep/flat ground
found no bowl deeper than 0.3 m (3 m rings and 6/10 m rings). `LotFromLaneTests` (every lane-fed lot connector: ends at the lane's height, ground at the join is the
lane's level, no hollow round it; 8 types x 5 grounds x 2 seeds) passes. The terraform log said `reached its target ... worst 0.54 m`, so a deep bowl is either in a target
the model does not produce or made by the game after the terraform (zoned building terraform, net embankment). Added:
* `TerraformPits.Fill` (Core/Build): a lot build raises any pit of the terraform target (lower than the lowest point of 5/9/13 m rings by > 0.35 m) to its rim.
  Log `pit guard: raised N texel(s) ... deepest bowl D m at (x, z)` — if this ever fires, the plan has the bowl and the cause is in the plan.
* `TerraformPits.Worst` after the build: `terrain check: bowl D m deep at (x, z); live ground .., target there .. (the game's ground differs from the target | the target itself has it)`.
  That line tells the next playtest which side makes the bowl.
Known unrelated failures kept as they were: 6 `MallEntranceTests` hollow cases (0.11-0.16 m at street corners on fill) and `LotGradingTests ... shopsPark/facingIn/steep`.
