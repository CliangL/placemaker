## Features (toggle inside a type)
- Restrooms (fix classification) · Kiosks / food stands / ice-cream cart · **small shops via zoning**:
  commercial zoning band along the park/plaza/lot edge facing streets, or "shop pads" (zoning patches inside)
- Central plaza · pond · fountain · playground · picnic · big trees · flowerbeds (exist)
- **Decals scatter**: pick decals (leaves, paving patterns, cracks, puddles, graffiti, markings) spread with density/ clustering
- **Terrain variation**: gentle mounds/hills, terraces, sunken lawn, berms along roads (noise-shaped, smooth, max slope), "flatten" for plazas
- Public art / statues · lookout tower · gazebo / bandstand · pergolas · sculpture trail
- Lighting style (classic lamps / modern / string lights) · Night lighting density
- Water features: stream (meandering channel), small waterfalls (terraform), reflecting pool
- Wildlife: bird houses, animal spawners (exists for park), insect hotels
- Bike paths & racks · bike-share dock · scooter parking
- Parking edges: EV chargers, trolley shelters, bike racks, attendant booth, toll gates
- Seasonal: autumn tree mix, flower colours
- Accessibility: ramps, accessible paths, seating every N m
